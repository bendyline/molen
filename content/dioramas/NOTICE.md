# Molen dioramas

Original procedural set pieces and choreography for Molen. MIT license, as in the repository.
The football teams and their colors are fictional. No external models or textures are required.

The Eastlake, Marymoor, market and community-field placement centers and orientations in `placements.json` are derived from the bundled
Protomaps/OpenStreetMap regional extract. © OpenStreetMap contributors, ODbL-1.0:
https://www.openstreetmap.org/copyright. Retain the map attribution when displaying these placements. The airport placement is an approximate authored demo; its reference is
listed in the placement catalog. All event themes and schedules are fictional.
