The source CSV and metadata are checksum-pinned in build-wildlife-ranges.py.
Changes: retain supported extant, non-domestic mammals; omit uncertain country
membership; normalize country names; retain country sets only. MDD includes native,
reintroduced native and ancient introductions, not most recent introductions.

Country grid derived from Natural Earth Admin 0 Map Units 1:50m, 5.1.1,
public domain. https://www.naturalearthdata.com/about/terms-of-use/
Changes: quarter-degree center-sampled raster; no coastal extrapolation.
Source boundaries are used only for coarse visual selection.

Country membership plus biome and mapped land cover describes potential habitat,
not observed animals, occupancy, population abundance, or a present-day census.
Within-country distribution is not represented. Large/rare mammals additionally
require mapped conservation habitat. Functional bird, reptile, fish and insect
groups have no asserted species identity. Habitat/density/body recipes are Molen
authoring choices, not data supplied by or endorsed by the source authors.
