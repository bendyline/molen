Derived by packages/worldgen-earth/scripts/build-architecture-boundaries.py and
generate-structure-atlas.mjs. Exterior rings simplified to 0.02 degrees and coordinates
rounded to 0.001 degrees. Coastlines, borders, and small islands are approximate.
A nearest-outline fallback up to 2.5 km in local world coordinates fills otherwise-unclassified
coastal gaps. Exact containment always wins. It does not place buildings on unmapped water.
Source outlines use Natural Earth's de facto boundary convention. Molen uses them only
as broad content-selection envelopes; they are not architectural surveys or political claims.
