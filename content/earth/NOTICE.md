# Earth content attribution

The region atlas and authored placement data use the repository's MIT license. The business
identity catalog includes BSD-3-Clause data from Name Suggestion Index; its complete copyright
and license notice is in [businesses/README.md](businesses/README.md).

Font outlines and their compiled subsets are licensed separately under SIL Open Font License
1.1: Overpass and Overpass Mono, copyright 2021 The Overpass Project Authors, and Molen Sign
Serif, derived from Libre Baskerville, copyright 2012 The Libre Baskerville Project Authors.
The derivative serif face has been renamed in accordance with the reserved-name terms.

Complete font notices: [Overpass](fonts/Overpass-OFL.txt),
[Libre Baskerville](fonts/LibreBaskerville-OFL.txt).
Source revisions, modifications and reproduction instructions: [fonts/README.md](fonts/README.md).

Road-sign country outlines in `signs/regions.atlas.json` derive from the same public-domain
Natural Earth country source as the architectural geography. See
[architecture-geography.NOTICE.txt](architecture-geography.NOTICE.txt) for provenance and
[signs/README.md](signs/README.md) for generation and national sign references. Sign conventions
are authored data; no proprietary typeface is redistributed.

U.S. state route artwork includes CC0-1.0 OpenStreetMap Americana vector symbols, adapted for
medium-fi signs, plus authored primitive shapes. See [signs/americana-LICENSE.txt](signs/americana-LICENSE.txt)
and [signs/README.md](signs/README.md) for pinned revisions, changes and reproduction commands.
The state boundary layer uses public-domain Natural Earth 1:50m admin-1 data; its upstream
revision, source URL and hash are retained in `source/signs/us-state-boundaries.json`.
