Molen regional ecology

ecoregions.json is derived from RESOLVE Ecoregions 2017 under CC BY 4.0.
Dinerstein et al. (2017), An Ecoregion-Based Approach to Protecting Half the
Terrestrial Realm, BioScience 67(6):534-545.
https://doi.org/10.1093/biosci/bix014
https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/4.0/
Source: https://storage.googleapis.com/teow2016/Ecoregions2017.zip
Dataset documentation and license:
https://developers.google.com/earth-engine/datasets/catalog/RESOLVE_ECOREGIONS_2017

Molen changes: rasterized ecological regions to a global quarter-degree grid,
retained intersecting coastal/island land where cell centers have no coverage,
removed unused attributes, and run-length encoded the rows. This is approximate
potential habitat geography, not current land cover or observed wildlife.
Source SHA-256 and exact interpretation are embedded in ecoregions.json.

Other original Molen content is MIT licensed.
