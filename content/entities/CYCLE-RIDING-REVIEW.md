# Cycle riding review

The six fleet cycles are now mountable vehicles: standard and cruiser motorcycles, a motor
scooter, and city, road and front-box cargo bicycles. The existing medium-fi models gained
articulated handlebars, bicycle cranks and level pedals, and motorcycle footrests. Geometry is
original and generated by `packages/entities/scripts/fleet-geometry.mjs` from each model's
`models/recipe.json`; no external models, textures or image-generation prompts were used.

## Handling and rider contacts

`vehicle.spec.cycle` selects two centreline tire contacts and assisted balance. The target lean
is `-atan2(speed * yawRate, gravity)`, bounded by `maxLean` and approached through the existing
damped suspension state. The lean limit also caps steering using `gravity * tan(maxLean)`;
tighter limits produce wider fast turns. Tire slip, braking, weather, terrain availability and
host collision probes remain part of the vehicle solver. Stock cycles brake instead of reversing.

The driver seat anchors the pelvis at the saddle. The shared figure pose evaluator places hands
on the steering grips and feet on the footrests or moving pedals. Pedal phase is deterministic
component state, advances under forward power and holds while coasting or braking. Wheel spin
continues while coasting. Riders leave upright through checked exits after stopping, and an
unoccupied cycle finishes settling upright.

At low-speed steering lock the rider counterweights at the saddle and turns the shoulders with
the bars, keeping both grips reachable. Steering fades at speed, leaving chassis lean dominant.

This is assisted, grounded riding: no falling, wheelies, airborne tricks, gears or crash damage.
The stock contact geometry fits an adult human; other body sizes may not reach every contact.
Cycles are selectable in the Earth view example but are excluded from automatic car parking and
ambient lane traffic. See [the vehicle guide](../../docs-src/guide/vehicles.md#riding-cycles).

## Visual and simulation review

Four 640×480 turntable views of every cycle were inspected after importing the articulated
models. Six 1000×800 mounted-rider views run 90 ticks under power with steering; a 1600×1000
gallery places all six riders on a lit, shadow-receiving plaza. The review checked silhouettes,
wheel/fork alignment, leaning rider and chassis, saddle placement, hand and foot contacts,
materials and shadows. Six additional 1000×800 views check the stopped riders at full steering
lock. Captures use antialiasing and reflections.

Reproduce the review after building the packages:

```sh
node packages/entities/scripts/capture-cycles.mjs --models
node packages/entities/scripts/capture-cycles.mjs --lock
node packages/tooling/dist/cli.mjs validate content/entities/scenes/cycles.scene.json
node packages/tooling/dist/cli.mjs sim run content/entities/scenes/cycles.scene.json --setup content/entities/scenes/cycles.setup.mjs --ticks 120 --hash
```

Ignored captures live in `source/cycles/<slug>/shots/angle_0.png` through `angle_3.png`,
`shots/<slug>-riding.png`, `shots/<slug>-steering-lock.png` and `shots/cycles.png`.
`shots/cycle-riding-report.json` and `shots/cycle-steering-lock-report.json` record exact
source hashes, simulation hashes and render statistics.
The six-rider gallery renders 12,988 triangles in 84 draw calls.

Automated coverage exercises all six types through mounting, acceleration, turning, braking and
safe dismount; symmetric lean and lean-limited turning; centreline terrain probes and missing
terrain; freewheeling and exact keyframe restoration; upright exits and idle balance. Figure
tests check all four contacts through eight crank positions at straight and both full steering
limits for every stock cycle. An Earth view
test selects and boards each type through the public API and then returns to walking.

retried with `pnpm assets:build --no-generate`, which reused completed outputs. The final asset
check verified all 2,348 GLBs as `assets-7fafb022c67e8b96`. The fleet source byte comparison also

- `pnpm test:golden`: all browser suites passed.
