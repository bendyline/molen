## Real aircraft designations

Two models depict real aircraft types and are identified by their public designations: the
P-51D Mustang (`molen.entities.aircraft.p51d`) and the OH-6 (`molen.entities.aircraft.oh6`).
The models are original low-poly interpretations; their flight tuning is game-model tuning, not a
reconstruction of flight-test data.
