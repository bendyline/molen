# Shaolin Monastery

Shaolin’s Changzhu Courtyard climbs the hillside from the Mountain Gate to the Hall of the Heavenly Kings, Mahavira Hall and the upper halls. The gate’s round lattice windows, sculpted stone guardians and open passage lead to the courtyard. Broad tiled roofs and red timber frame the stepped procession.
