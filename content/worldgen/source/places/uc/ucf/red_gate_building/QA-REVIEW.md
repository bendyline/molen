# Red Gate Building review

All four detail levels were rendered in Molen with PMTiles buildings and roads and standard Terrarium elevation. The model replaces its 28 building records and retains 30 surrounding buildings, the native terrain and the mapped roads. The rear court is open at all six sample points. Rays pass through both tested shoulder loggias at street and close-up. At the sampled approach, the outer step is 13.4 cm above the pavement. The failed-load test restored all 28 footprints, and unloading released the model geometry.

The source master, imported master and four detail levels passed Khronos glTF validation. The scene validated and completed a headless simulation tick. The detail levels use five shared material graphs and contain 754, 3,558, 15,834 and 46,124 triangles. Skyline and district together are 376 KB before HTTP compression.

Inspected 68 views, including the Garden Ring portico and the east-side Metro door bank at the mapped sidewalk elevation. Flat preview fixtures lie above the Metro thresholds, so the native captures establish the exterior fit. The crest, star, ornamental trim and individual doors are reconstructed from photographs.
