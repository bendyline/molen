# Leningradskaya Hotel review

All four detail levels were rendered in Molen with PMTiles buildings and roads and standard Terrarium elevation. The hotel replaces its 14 mapped building records while retaining 23 independent nearby buildings and the surrounding roads. The recessed rear court stays open at all four levels. At the sampled approach, the lowest step is within 7 cm of ground level. The failed-load test restored all 14 mapped records, and unloading released the model geometry.

The source master, imported master and four derivatives passed Khronos glTF validation. Shared limestone, brick, granite and metal surfaces loaded from the existing material library. The four derivatives contain 997, 3,421, 15,633 and 27,417 triangles respectively. Skyline and district together are 379 KB before HTTP compression.

Inspected 61 renders covering the signed entrance, roof crown, rear wings, material changes across distances and the native setting. Window profiles, ornamental carving and the top emblem are approximate. See the [site preview](site-preview.png) and [hash-bound review](qa.json).
