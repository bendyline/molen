# Ministry of Foreign Affairs main building review

All four detail levels were rendered in Molen using PMTiles buildings and roads and standard Terrarium elevation. The model replaced 196 records for the main complex while retaining 83 neighboring records, including the separate northern annex. The tested side and rear clearances stayed open. The lowest step was within 13 cm of grade at all three sampled points across the portico. A failed load restored the mapped complex, and unloading released the model geometry.

The source master, imported master and four detail levels passed Khronos glTF validation. The scene validated and completed a headless simulation tick. Four shared material graphs supply the limestone, granite, painted metal and copper. The levels contain 892, 3,988, 15,654 and 36,142 triangles. Skyline and district together are 408 KB before HTTP compression.

Inspected 65 renders of the entrance, facade ribs, stone crest, silver spire, stepped wings and rounded rear range. Fine relief, windows and some service elevations are approximate. See the [site preview](site-preview.png) and [hash-bound review](qa.json).
