# Hotel Ukraina

The [model recipe](models/ukraina-model.mjs) reconstructs Moscow’s Hotel Ukraina and attached residential wings as an independent Seven Sisters asset. [The map frame](map-frame.json) supplies the occupied outlines, roof stages, orientation and entrance grade. Photographs inform the arched bays, roof urns, entrance canopy and star-tipped spire.

Run `node packages/worldgen/scripts/generate-hotel-ukraina.mjs` to reproduce the source GLB. The master and four streaming levels share limestone, granite, painted metal and copper.

The entrance drive follows the sampled viewer terrain and joins the covered arrival platform. The rear court stays open between the attached wings. Fine masonry, window profiles, roof ornament and canopy trim are approximate. The exterior depicts the permanent architecture.
