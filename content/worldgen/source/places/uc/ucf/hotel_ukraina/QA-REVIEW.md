# Hotel Ukraina review

All four detail levels rendered at the mapped riverfront site with PMTiles and Terrarium elevation. Each replaced the hotel and residential wings’ 63 building records, retained eight neighbors, and left the rear court open. The driveway meets the sampled grade within 7 cm at 18 points across its width. Small interpolated patches of terrain show through the side of the paving. The level landing stays above sampled grade. The center route below the canopy stayed open in the geometry test. A failed load restored the mapped buildings, and unloading released the model’s geometry.

The source master, imported master and all four detail levels passed Khronos glTF validation. The scene passed schema validation and completed a headless tick. The levels use four shared materials and contain 836, 3,878, 15,840 and 58,414 triangles. Skyline and district total 386 KB before HTTP compression.

Inspected 65 views of the stepped limestone tower, attached wings, rear court, entrance canopy, roof urns and spire. Fine ornament and the forecourt grades are approximate. See the [site preview](site-preview.png) and [hash-bound review](qa.json).
