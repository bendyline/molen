# Kudrinskaya Square Building review

All four detail levels were rendered in Molen using PMTiles buildings and roads and standard Terrarium elevation. The model replaces its 25 mapped building records while preserving 44 neighboring buildings and the square. Both recessed approaches remain open. The first sampled step sits within 12 cm of the front grade and 6 cm of the rear grade. The tested wing arches stay open at street and close-up. A failed load restored the 25 mapped records, and unloading released the model geometry.

The checked source master, imported master and four detail levels passed Khronos glTF validation. The scene validated and completed one headless simulation tick. Five shared material graphs loaded through the existing library. The four levels contain 774, 3,714, 14,300 and 30,494 triangles. Skyline and district together are 366 KB before HTTP compression.

Inspected 65 renders of the podium, open galleries, portals, wing setbacks, lantern and spire. The door relief, precise window profiles and star are approximations from photographs. See the [site preview](site-preview.png) and [hash-bound review](qa.json).
