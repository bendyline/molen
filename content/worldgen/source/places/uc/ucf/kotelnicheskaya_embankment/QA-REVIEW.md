# Kotelnicheskaya Embankment Building review

All four detail levels rendered at the confluence with PMTiles and Terrarium elevation. They replaced 19 building records across two vector tiles and retained 54 neighbors. The nine courtyard probes stayed open. The entrance paving sits 9 to 18 cm above sampled terrain and meets the three entrance treads. Roads, river geometry and land cover remained in place. The failed-load test restored all 19 mapped records, and unloading released the model geometry.

The source master, imported master and four streaming levels passed Khronos glTF validation. The scene passed schema validation and completed a headless tick. The 109,656-triangle master and the four streaming levels share granite, limestone, painted metal and copper. Streaming levels contain 936, 3,066, 15,326 and 56,572 triangles. Skyline and district total 317 KB before HTTP compression.

Inspected 65 views, including both riverside ranges, the stepped tower with three radial wings, open shoulder arcades and the copper spire. Crown sculpture, balcony details and courtyard service facades are approximations. See the [site preview](site-preview.png) and [hash-bound review](qa.json).
