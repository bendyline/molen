# Diyarbakır Mardin Gate and the Southwest Walls review

Mardin Kapı opens through the south curtain. Two projecting round bastions, Yedi Kardeş and Evli Beden, rise along the sloping westward rampart. [Overview](preview.png)

Six GLBs passed glTF validation with zero errors, warnings or embedded images. Skyline and district total 140,880 bytes. [QA record](qa.json)
