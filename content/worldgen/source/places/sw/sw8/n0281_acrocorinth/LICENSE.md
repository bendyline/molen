# Acrocorinth model data and geometry

The adapted archaeological wall data and this asset's generated geometry are licensed under
[Creative Commons Attribution-ShareAlike4.0](https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by-sa/4.0/).
Credit:James A.Herbst,Corinth Excavations,American School of Classical Studies at Athens.
Changes:ellipsoidal projection conversion,local-frame rotation,rounded coordinates,selected
and simplified courses,estimated heights/openings and medium-fi levels. No ASCSA endorsement.

The terrain grid is produced using Copernicus data and information funded by the European
Union - EU-DEM layers,through Mapzen/Terrain Tiles. Source documentation and attribution
links are in relief-grid.json and reference-metadata.json. Samples are interpreted into
a relative terrain patch and are not a surveyed replacement.

OSM site identity and cached frame:© OpenStreetMap contributors,ODbL-1.0. That boundary
is not extruded into walls. Source generator code and shared material graphs retain their
repository licenses. Official research photographs remain external links and do not ship.
