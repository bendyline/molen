# Grand Bazaar, Isfahan

Completed as eleven independently imported and geographically placed structures. The local manifest `collection.json` lists the owned mapped route, markets and courts. Each member retains its own source, four detail levels, QA, placement and Qualla story annotation. There is no aggregate runtime model.

The signed original source outlines and route controls establish horizontal scope. Widths, roof coverage, roof-bay census, unobserved courts/storeys/facades and ground contact retain the estimates documented by each member. This is an approximate architectural visualization, not a measured modern roof survey.

`route-coverage.json` records123 bounded2D checks and zero remaining local gap. The separately assigned Jameh Mosque and its open approaches remain external; their original ownership proof is under `native-map/`. This local collection is separate from the next1000 catalog.

`collection-review.json` binds existing individual approvals, the two inspected collective views, the unchanged2048m original terrain and the assembly. No accepted member was reauthored or recaptured for this review. Bulk images stay in ignored local artifacts.

Regenerate the scene with `node content/worldgen/source/places/tj/tju/grand_bazaar_isfahan/generate-collection-scene.mjs`; use `--check` to verify saved outputs.
