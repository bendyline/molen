# Esagila reconstruction review

The isolated reconstruction shows the open main courtyard, east entrance annex, white lime-plastered facades, horizontal roofs and fired brick paving. Six GLBs preserve a clear central court of about 31 × 36 m and seven modeled gateway thresholds. The roof elevations, western sanctuary and the annex interiors are interpretive. [Temple preview](preview.png) · [Courtyard preview](courtyard-preview.webp)

All six GLBs passed validation with zero warnings, errors or embedded images. Skyline and district together are 328,996 bytes. All four runtime levels drew at their intended distance transitions and released their geometry on unload. [QA record](qa.json)

Runtime review uses the PMTiles excavation point, the approximate survey-plan bearing and 29.85 m floor. The historical grade clears the occupied site and preserves all mapped neighboring buildings sampled. The default view and both out-of-range dates use the original terrain and load zero temple assets. The historical terrain is a bounded visualization using the excavated floor datum.
