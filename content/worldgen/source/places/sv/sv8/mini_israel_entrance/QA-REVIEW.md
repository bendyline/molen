# Mini Israel entrance and workshop review

The inspected views show two open pointed arches over the approach, the steel canopy, limestone wings and the glazed upper pavilion. The park-side façade has glazed café bays, round openings, and a curved terrace with blue fascia and a dark railing. The geometry follows the photograph from GMA Architects. The railing opens at the mapped footbridge. [Model preview](preview.png) · [Earth placement preview](site-preview.webp)

The footprint and access road place the entrance at the real park beside the roundabout, with both arches toward the approach. The four detail levels replace only the matching footprint. The two sampled slab clearances are 0.5 and 37.2 cm over the normal viewer terrain. The terrace remains about 0.84 m landward of the PMTiles water boundary. The terrace grade, window spacing and exact bridge transition are estimates. [Placement checks](placement-report.json) · [PMTiles and terrain](pmtiles-fit.json)

The skyline and district levels total 283,512 bytes. All six GLBs passed validation without warnings or embedded images. A failed download kept the original map building. Successful loads preserved the other five map buildings, roads, water and terrain. Unload released the model and restored the scene. [QA record](qa.json)
