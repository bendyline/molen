# Mini Israel · Orot Rabin Power Station exhibit

This miniature reproduces the one-stack display at Mini Israel in Latrun. The stack, inclined conveyor and low retaining wall read at the skyline level. Closer levels add boiler bracing, conveyor supports, coal lumps, vehicle silhouettes and visitor-area markings. The exhibit placard carries the two boards, the number 49 badge and Hebrew and English lettering from the 2003 photo. The lettering is geometry typeset with DejaVu Sans Bold and Libre Baskerville. Shared material graphs provide the concrete, painted metal and stone surfaces. [2003 exhibit placard](https://jr.co.il/pictures/israel/mini-israel/mi051.htm) · [DejaVu font license](https://dejavu-fonts.github.io/License.html)

Four views recorded in November 2011 place the chimney east of the visitor plaza. The clearest view puts the shaft at about 4 m high, and two views put both boiler roofs near 2.1 m. The archive listing was published in May 2012. [Reference observations](reference-observations.json) · [Archived panorama](https://virtualglobetrotting.com/map/mini-israel-power-plant/view/google/)

The world viewer places this exhibit for dates in November 2011. The apron faces approximately 262° true and clears the nearest mapped visitor path by 1.21 m.
