# Mini Israel · Ben Gurion Airport exhibit

The 2012 apron has seven miniature airliners, parking circles, a dark guide track, and a low rail. The blue widebody and raised yellow DHL freighter read from the archived viewing direction. Their exact types and dimensions are estimates. [Archive panorama](https://virtualglobetrotting.com/map/mini-israel-airplane-models/view/google/) · [Overview](preview.png) · [Aircraft detail](aircraft-preview.webp)

All six GLBs passed validation. Each level has all seven aircraft and the apron. The first two levels total 219,616 bytes. The two shared material graphs supply painted metal and concrete. [QA record](qa.json)

The initial trial left the southwest edge 1.5 m above the ground. The registered 2012 view uses a date-specific grade, and the apron now clears the nearest mapped path by 2.77 m. All 61 sampled apron points met the terrain in the four detail tiers and on the two coarser test grids. The sampled paving elevations varied by less than 8 mm. The model and grade are active for the panorama’s publication date, 17 May 2012. The old grade, exact dimensions, and present-day display remain uncertain.
