# Mini Israel outdoor amphitheater review

The model retains the open stage frame and black back wall, terraced seating, two aisles and masonry retaining walls. The projected outline and seating direction match the photographed amphitheater. Seats and equipment detail are interpreted. [Venue preview](preview.png) · [Park aerial](https://www.the7eye.org.il/460149)

Khronos validation passed for the source, imported model and four detail levels. The skyline and district files total 248,640 bytes. The GLBs share ashlar, concrete and painted metal and carry no embedded images. [QA record](qa.json)

Overhead imagery and the oblique park photograph fix the stage facing and the terrace footprint. The viewer rendered the placement at one- and two-metre terrain grid steps and kept the west path open. The 175.35 m datum is approximate. The model kept the existing water, ground and land cover.
