# Mini Israel · Dizengoff Center exhibit

The miniature has a round gray tower with projecting balconies, a rust-red tower with pale mullions, and a stepped limestone podium. The proportions and roof details follow the visitor photograph. Individual balcony fittings, facade panels and the podium underside are estimates. [Photographs](https://www.israel-in-photos.com/mini-israel.html)

Three views from 2012 locate the two shafts and establish their order and approximate bearing. The placement is available on the archive's published date, 17 May 2012. The current display and exact podium outline remain unverified. [Archive panorama](https://virtualglobetrotting.com/map/mini-israel-tel-aviv/view/google/) · [Map frame](map-frame.json)

The master and four viewer tiers share Molen's concrete, limestone, window and painted metal material graphs.
