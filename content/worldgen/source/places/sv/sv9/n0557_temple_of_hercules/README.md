# Temple of Hercules

![Molen preview](preview.png)

Standing Temple of Hercules at Amman: two tall plain segmented shafts with original simplified Corinthian leafy capitals and short broken lintel, four broken uncapped shafts at distinct heights, molded bases/local rubble footings. Low interrupted cella/perimeter masonry, occupied earthen portico mound/graded front approach and a few fallen drums/cornice fragments. Existing shared Corinthian capital/base geometry is adapted without its fluted shaft. No intact cella/roof, restored missing column rows, copied inscription/sculpture atlas or separate hand-of-Hercules artifact. Museum, Roman Temenos, watchtower, sculpture garden and surrounding city remain independent.
