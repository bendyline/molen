# Victims of Acts of Terror Memorial

Two Jerusalem limestone walls carry rows of bronze name plaques around an open court. A sculpted tower with four pierced faces rises above the bronze flame and fan-shaped paving. The terrace joins the mapped walking routes at both ends.

Run `node packages/worldgen/scripts/generate-mount-herzl-memorial.mjs` to rebuild the detailed master. The landmark LOD generator exports four levels. Shared limestone, concrete and bronze use metric UVs.

OpenStreetMap via Protomaps supplies the pedestrian area, memorial point and adjoining paths. Aerial imagery identifies the wall baselines. The court and apertures follow exterior photos. The bronze plaques use abstract engraving and omit individual names.
