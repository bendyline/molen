# Holocaust History Museum, Yad Vashem

The hillside museum has a buried triangular spine, narrow glazed ridge, railed entrance bridge, stepped exit, west overlook and integrated Hall of Names roof light. [Campus aerial](https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:Yad_Vashem_View.jpg) · [West overlook photograph](https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:ExteriorHolocaustHistoryMuseumYadVashemMar112023_08.jpg) · [Entrance photograph](https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:Yad_Vashem_entrance.jpg)

All four levels replace one mapped museum footprint and the two bridge lines that lead to it. The eastern deck meets the native footpath within 0.2 cm and the western flight meets its lower path within 2.9 cm. Six independent buildings and 66 transportation records remain in the test tile. The native road and footpath cross above the two buried sections between skylight runs. In the wider street-level fixture all ten Yad Vashem members loaded together. A failed museum load preserves both native bridge lines and its native building. Unloading releases the model geometry.

The central ridge and access grades follow the coarse standard terrain. The count of west stairs, exact roof grades, local retaining landscape and rail dimensions are reconstructed from photographs. The integrated Hall of Names roof position and rim profile are approximate. Underground rooms, exhibitions and the visitor route inside the museum remain outside the exterior scope.
