# Jisr ed Damiye

The closed Bailey span at Damiyah has paired green steel trusses, a timber deck, concrete abutments and locked gates at both ends.

Run `node packages/worldgen/scripts/generate-damiye-bridge.mjs` to rebuild the detailed master. The landmark LOD generator exports four levels. Four shared material graphs cover board-formed concrete, timber, painted steel and galvanized metal.

The deck follows the named PMTiles bridge line and connects to the native approaches. The crossing is depicted in its closed state. Deck and bearing dimensions are estimated from photos and terrain.
