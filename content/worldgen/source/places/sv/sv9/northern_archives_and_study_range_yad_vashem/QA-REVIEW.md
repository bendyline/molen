# Northern archives and study range, Yad Vashem

The northern Yad Vashem range follows its bent PMTiles footprint. The western seminar glazing bands, recessed limestone library windows, school piers and glazed stair towers remain distinct across the joined volumes. [Library exterior](https://tafnit-eng.com/en/project/yad-vashem-archives-and-library-building/) · [School exterior](https://tafnit-eng.com/en/project/international-school-for-holocaust-studies-yad-vashem/) · [Seminars wing](https://www.ibtimes.co.uk/yad-vashem-new-building-holocaust-education-289787)

All four detail levels preserve the terrain and replace the one matching PMTiles building. Seven nearby structures, including the detached wing, and 65 transportation records stay visible as native context. Failure preserves the original building. Unloading releases model geometry. Five roof control points pass in each detail level.

The assignment of exterior bays uses relative position in the overhead and exterior photographs. Roof heights, opening counts, roof equipment and the uphill facade remain approximate. This model covers the connected range. The detached study wing still needs its own exterior model.
