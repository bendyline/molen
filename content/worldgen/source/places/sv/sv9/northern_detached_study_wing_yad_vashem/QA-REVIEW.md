# Administration and Research Building, Yad Vashem

The Administration and Research Building follows the detached footprint southeast of the archives range. Its raised west roof, four grouped north-facing windows, smaller punched openings and central roof access bulkhead are individually modeled. The visitor map puts this building opposite the archives and library. [Visitor map](https://naphtali.smugmug.com/Travel/ISRAEL/ISRAEL-9-Yad-Vashem) · [North aerial](https://www.traveldojo.com/yad-vashem/) · [South aerial](https://www.giorgioperlasca.it/yad-vashem/)

All four detail levels replace the matching PMTiles building and preserve the other six buildings, terrain and 69 transportation records. The close-up fixture also loaded both northern models together. The two footprints were replaced independently and the five shared material graphs resolved once each. Failure restores the native building and unload releases the model geometry.

Roof heights, the exact pattern of smaller windows and west entrance treads are estimated from the aerial photographs and standard terrain. The model covers the exterior.

Six GLBs validated and 65 current views were inspected. Skyline and district total 206,752 bytes. [Site preview](site-preview.png) · [QA record](qa.json)
