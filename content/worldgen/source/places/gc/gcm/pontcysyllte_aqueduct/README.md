# Pontcysyllte Aqueduct

Nineteen cast-iron spans carry the Llangollen Canal above the Dee. Tapering ashlar piers meet the valley below, with pointed cutwaters in the river. The narrow east towpath follows the trough to both landings. [Reference observations](reference-observations.json)

The source generator rebuilds the master GLB. Four authored detail levels use the shared material library. [Site controls](site-controls.json)

The World Viewer uses a bounded sampling grid derived from Welsh Government LiDAR to recover the surveyed approach grades and Dee valley. The bridge’s iron trough supplies the elevated crossing water. The River Dee retains its mapped water geometry. [Terrain and placement review](QA-REVIEW.md)

Terrain attribution is Welsh Government under the Open Government Licence. The site controls record the source tiles, hashes and OSTN15 transformation. The DTM’s filtered water samples represent the river surface and give no bathymetry. [DataMapWales LiDAR](https://datamap.gov.wales/maps/lidar-data-download/)
