# Pontcysyllte Aqueduct review

Nineteen open arches between 18 tapered stone piers support the iron trough and east towpath. Four river piers have cutwaters. Close views show the riveted trough, supporting ribs, water channel and balustrade. [Overview](preview.png)

Six GLBs passed glTF validation with zero errors, warnings or embedded images. Skyline and district total 316,120 bytes. [QA record](qa.json)

The model follows the actual canal and towpath. On the survey-backed terrain grid, the two deck ends meet the mapped landings within 0.19 m and the deck is 38.34 m above the river sample. The renderer suppresses the inferred towpath span and preserves unrelated bridges and native river geometry. The geographic check used a rotated, blended grid resampled from Welsh Government LiDAR.
