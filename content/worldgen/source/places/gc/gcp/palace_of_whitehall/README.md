# Palace of Whitehall

This historical reconstruction depicts both sides of Whitehall around 1670. The east side includes the linked palace courts, Privy Garden frontage, Great Hall, Chapel Royal, river lodgings and gates. The park side includes the Real Tennis Court, Cockpit playhouse, Tiltyard, Horse Guard Yard and service ranges.

The two plans are aligned to the surviving Jacobean hall. The independently traced Holbein Gate differs by 0.77 m between the plans near the hall. Heights, chimneys, masonry openings and the historic river stairs include estimates. The 1670 model uses the hall's Jacobean outline. The present-day hall has its own catalog asset.

Pass `viewingDate: '1670-07-01'` to `mountEarthView` for the representative palace snapshot. Its local Thames shoreline, approximate water level and site grades use the same date interval.
