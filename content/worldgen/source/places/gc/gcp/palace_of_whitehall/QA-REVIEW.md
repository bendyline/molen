# Palace of Whitehall review

The east precinct retains the open Privy Garden and Palace Court, the Holbein passage, the high-roofed hall and chapel, the Jacobean Banqueting House, and the river lodgings. The west shows the Cockpit, Tennis Court and two open yards. The garden paths and lawns meet without overlapping surfaces.
