# Farringdon Railway Station

Farringdon’s surface interchange includes the heritage Cowcross headhouse, modern hall, two glazed canopy families, the low western ticket hall and lift shafts, Bloom and the complete Kaleidoscope host at Long Lane. The signed footprint follows PMTiles. The Cowcross photographs and the current Bloom aerial guided the western hall frontage. [Station photographs](https://www.jra.co.uk/projects/bloom-clerkenwell)

The five approach aprons meet native paving or terrain within 3.4 cm at 15 sampled joins across all four detail levels. The four sampled rail gaps remain open. The viewer replaces 20 mapped station records and retains all 773 neighboring buildings and 873 transportation inputs. Failed loads keep the original station, and unloading restores the original ground and frees the model geometry.

The short thresholds, aprons and lobby recesses approximate current street grades using the standard host terrain and PMTiles pavement. Canopy profiles and underground rail levels are visual estimates. The western low ticket hall roof and Bloom height are inferred from current street and aerial photographs. The model shows the above-ground building envelopes and recessed doors. Platform interiors, escalators, train service and street furnishings use the host scene.
