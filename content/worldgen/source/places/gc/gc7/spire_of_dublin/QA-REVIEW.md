## Source and placement

Its smaller approximate 2.2 m ring and 121.2 m map height are used only for identity and center; published 3 m base and 120 m height control geometry. Heading0 maps native+X east,+Z south. ## Accepted reconstruction limits

Primary AQUA fabricator, IMOA engineering article and official Failte Ireland tourism references support 120 m height, 3 m base, 15 cm tip, first10m partly polished artwork and exactly11,884 15mm holes in upper12m. The architect-credited IMOA base photograph was inspected for geological horizontal islands, concentric grating, stone surround and bollards; the AQUA whole-spire image was inspected for silhouette. Detailed source remains byte-identical. Browser close-up replaces through-cut tip holes with opaque daylight diffuser marks; street/district average subpixel holes. Base artwork is an original reconstruction, not an exact tracing; bollard phase, maintenance hatches, current paving, night beacon and wind animation are outside this static daylight scope.
