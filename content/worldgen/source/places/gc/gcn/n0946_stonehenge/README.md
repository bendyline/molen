# Stonehenge — N0946

![Current lit preview](preview.png)

Complete static medium-fi interpretation of the current surviving Stonehenge ensemble: seventeen outer sarsen uprights including the short fragment, six surviving outer lintels, three complete inner trilithons, two isolated inner uprights, fallen sarsen fragments, visible bluestones and stumps, a mostly obscured Altar Stone, Heel Stone, Slaughter Stone, both surviving Station Stones and low bank/ditch earthwork surface. Missing ancient stones and lintels are not reconstructed. Layout and condition were read from the current English Heritage2015 plan; above-ground profiles, heights, local tilts, fragmentation and joint dimensions are authored photographic interpretations, not survey meshes or exact stone-number identification. The first draft recipe remains unchanged and independently reproducible. Four browser levels retain every authored stone and the bank; chamfering and surface tessellation increase toward closeup. A shared sandstone graph stands in for sarsen silicified sandstone, and common raw basalt stands in for mixed bluestone geology. Lichen, carvings, graffiti and particular mineral grains are not copied. Plan controls and raw OSM stone outlines disagree locally because generalized aerial top outlines are not ground surveys; four distinct outlying stones constrain a rigid fit with no scale change. Physical phone/laptop timing is unmeasured.
