Q. How can I obtain permission to publish an image from the library's collections?

The Huntington Library does not require researchers to request permission to quote from or publish material that is in the public domain or for which we do not own the copyright, nor do we charge permissions fees. 

It is the researcher's responsibility to identify the copyright holder, if there is one, and obtain any necessary permission. Depending on your area of specialization, the following resources may prove useful to your copyright research: 

  • Copyright term and public domain in the United States (Cornell University Copyright Information Center) 
  • Copyright and Fair Use (Stanford University Libraries) 
  • WATCH File (Harry Ransom Center, University of Texas at Austin) 
  • Crown Copyright (The National Archives, United Kingdom) 
  • Publishing restrictions, when available, are listed in the library catalog or the "Restrictions" note of a collection's finding aid. 

For more detail, visit our Rights and Permissions page. 

Although we do not require permission for your request, the library does ask to be credited. When crediting or citing Huntington materials, please refer to the library's citation guide.
