# Seville Cathedral

The Giralda rises from the corner of the open orange-tree court. A brick shaft with lozenge relief supports open bells, narrowing Renaissance stages, and the bronze Giraldillo. The cathedral retains its crossing pinnacles, side chapels, roof terraces and flying buttresses. [Reference observations](reference-observations.json)
