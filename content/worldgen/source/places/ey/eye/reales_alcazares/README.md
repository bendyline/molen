# Reales Alcázares

Seville’s fortified Lion Gate, the Mudéjar Palace of Pedro I above the Patio de las Doncellas, Gothic Palace, and the garden galleries at the Mercury Pool.

The three principal palace courts, the fortified entry and the garden gallery fit PMTiles. The courtyards retain open arcades and modeled basins.

Run the Reales Alcázares generator registered in asset-build.json to rebuild the detailed source. The four detail levels share masonry, marble, clay tile, lime plaster, timber, mosaic and paving. Carving and color patterns are interpreted from photographs.
