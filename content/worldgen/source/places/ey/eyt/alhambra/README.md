# Alhambra

Granada’s hilltop Alcazaba, the Nasrid palaces and their Lions and Myrtles courts, the circular palace of Charles V, and the Partal and fortified towers.

The fort, Nasrid complex, Charles V Palace and Partal follow PMTiles. The separate Generalife model covers the gardens across the ravine.

Run the Alhambra generator registered in asset-build.json to rebuild the detailed source. The four detail levels use shared ashlar, lime plaster, marble, timber, tile and mosaic materials. The courtyard floors and wall heights are reconstructed from the map, terrain and photographs.
