# Alcazaba of Almería and Cerro de San Cristóbal

Almería’s hilltop Alcazaba, with its rounded Christian bastions, crenellated taifa garden enclosures, Muro de la Vela, and stepped Jayrán wall to Cerro de San Cristóbal.

Eight mapped footprints and the surrounding PMTiles paths and garden outline place the complex along the hill. Three gate passages and the Christian castle court stay open.

Run the Alcazaba generator registered in asset-build.json to rebuild the source. The four detail levels reuse lime plaster, ashlar, rough sandstone, timber and roof tiles.
