# Lorsch Abbey Torhalle and church fragment

The Torhalle has three continuous open passages, patterned red sandstone panels, slim pilasters, slate roofs, two side stair turrets and an open bell turret. The surviving three bays of the church include the buttressed west gable, Gothic window and shallow aisle arches.

Run `node packages/worldgen/scripts/generate-lorsch-abbey.mjs --ids=lorsch_abbey_torhalle_and_church` to rebuild the source master. The landmark LOD generator exports four runtime levels. This is an independently placed member of the Abbey and Altenmünster of Lorsch.

OpenStreetMap via Protomaps supplies the mapped site controls. Standard Terrarium elevations set the 104.1 m datum. The two surviving buildings follow their PMTiles footprints. Façade and roof profiles are estimated from the exterior photographs and passage detail. The model includes the three open Torhalle passages and the church exterior. Medieval buildings known through archaeology remain outside the standing architectural model.
