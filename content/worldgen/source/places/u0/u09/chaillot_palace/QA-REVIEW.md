# Chaillot Palace — completed exterior

Reviewed 2026-11-01. The model includes both curved wings, the two central and two terminal pavilions, four open colonnades, recessed closed glazing, stepped roofs with inset glazed wells, the mapped five-meter service annex, and the lower theater terrace. The upper central view axis stays open. Detailed tiers retain 8/8/4/4 columns; the skyline uses four supports per colonnade.

Placement uses exact OpenStreetMap relation 6826569, anchor `[2.288445526, 48.86213832]`, and heading `0.801120653744` radians. +X runs northeast along the composition; +Z faces southeast toward the gardens and Eiffel Tower. The production renderer replaces seven owned components (315–319, 345 and 346), retaining all 340 independent neighbors, including the aquarium and Palais d’Iéna. Two occupied wing outlines, the interpreted theater footprint, and four bounded approaches form seven ground cutouts.

The detailed source has **32,689 triangles / 3,926,536 bytes**. Browser tiers contain **657 / 3,064 / 8,799 / 16,077 triangles**. Initial skyline plus district download is **334,560 bytes**; close-up is **1,444,768 bytes**. Five existing shared 256² texture graphs—limestone, granite, gravel, painted metal and window grid—serve six material slots. There are no embedded or private images, and GPU vertex streams are separate.

Four focused checks pass: source/runtime/input pins and signed placement; curved-wing and pavilion roofs, the low annex and theater, 72 column-gap rays, closed glazing behind every street/close-up column gap, recessed glazed roof wells, continuous buried portico plinths and native approach contact; actual production replacement and neighbor retention; and exact LOD reconstruction, material bindings and budgets. All six GLBs pass Khronos validation with zero errors and warnings. All **46 final renders** were inspected: 10 portable, 10 shared-material/map, and 26 medium-fi terrain/context/lighting/LOD views. Hashes and commands are in `qa.json`.

During construction, the production check identified three additional roof/service components, the terminal glazing was aligned to the actual column gaps, and native terrain views exposed missing support beneath the portico slabs. Continuous buried plinths now close those gaps and have regression probes. Unloading disposes model geometry and restores the terrain cutout.

Three unmodified standard Terrarium tiles supply approximate terrain, using a 66 m datum, 67.3 m central floors and 61.6 m terminal floors. Curved-wing levels interpolate between them; four approaches sample the native field across their width. This is an approximate global-terrain fit, not a historic survey. The lower theater footprint, roof-well depth, rear details and window inventories are picture/map interpretations. Synthetic context does not represent the actual surrounding city. Sculpture, bas-reliefs, inscriptions, flags and interiors are excluded. Reference pictures stay local and are not textures. Physical-device timings and numerical LOD errors remain unmeasured; category activation is separate project work.
