# Catacombs of Paris · Avenue René-Coty exit

Recessed white Corian exit, glass canopy, stone flanking walls and a low roof set into the mapped street frontage. [Paris Musées reference](https://catacombes.paris.fr/histoire-du-lieu)

The address and museum photograph identify the low stone bays and recessed white frame facing Avenue René-Coty. The signed direction follows the mapped front building line and the named avenue. [Map frame](map-frame.json)

Wall depth, small façade hardware and ground heights are reconstructed. The adjacent streets and other buildings stay in the standard map layers.
The public surface building is separate from the quarry galleries and underground visitor itinerary.
