# Centre Georges Pompidou

The Centre Pompidou exterior retains the exposed white frame, six rises of the west escalator, red stair towers and color-coded services. Its paved piazza descends to the recessed Forum entrance. The central granite paving follows the museum’s documented 11% descent. [Museum description](https://www.centrepompidou.fr/en/pompidou-plus/magazine/article/architectural-secrets-the-piazza-of-the-centre-pompidou) · [Piazza photograph](https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:Place_Georges-Pompidou,_Paris_14_August_2013.jpg)

All four detail levels replace the mapped museum and preserve 436 neighboring building records, all 304 transportation records and the native water in the test tile. Six lateral probes include steep reconstructed transitions near the Brancusi Studio and native curb heights on the service and southern edges. A failed load retains the original museum and terrain.
