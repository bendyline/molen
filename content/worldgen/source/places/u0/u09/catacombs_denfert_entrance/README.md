# Catacombs of Paris · Denfert-Rochereau entrance

Ledoux pavilion with three rusticated arches and a hipped slate roof, beside the separate black-framed modern visitor entrance. [Paris Musées reference](https://www.catacombes.paris.fr/sites/default/files/2026-04/Catacombes_Dossier%20presse2026_WEB.pdf)

The entrance photograph matches the paved plaza face of the pavilion and the low building on its right. The direction is signed from the corresponding edge of the PMTiles footprint. [Map frame](map-frame.json)

Wall depth, small façade hardware and ground heights are reconstructed. The adjacent streets and other buildings stay in the standard map layers.
The public surface building is separate from the quarry galleries and underground visitor itinerary.
