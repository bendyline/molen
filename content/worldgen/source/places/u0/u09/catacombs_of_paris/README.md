# Catacombs of Paris

The model includes the Ledoux pavilion and modern entrance at Denfert-Rochereau, the Avenue René-Coty exit, and a draft of the public underground galleries. The plan uses east, up and south in metres. The origin is the mapped centre of the entrance pavilion.

Seven materials use the Worldgen shared library. The footbath water uses vertex colors. The authoring recipe generates the detailed master and all four runtime levels. The skyline level shows the public street buildings and the former Fontis exit. The nearer levels include the quarry passages, spiral stairs and handrails, the arched descent to the lower quarry, the painted ossuary gate, the sepulchral lamp, the September Martyrs’ wall, the Samaritaine well and the pillar in the Crypt of the Passion. The Samaritaine well has a stepped limestone rim, a recessed basin and a curved iron guard at street and close range. Both public stairs meet open arches at the lower galleries and open landings at the modeled surface thresholds. A separate, approximately 247 m event gallery connects the ossuary to the Salle des Fontis. The chamber includes the rough high vault, stone table, chevron floor and side vestibule shown in the museum brochure. The master and close-up reproduce the plan’s 83 spiral steps, and the street model uses a continuous spiral over the same rise. The upper landing reaches sampled ground elevation behind the low ashlar frontage at 36 rue Rémy-Dumoncel. The draft uses an estimated gallery grade and keeps the former street door closed. The passage beyond the upper landing remains unresolved.
