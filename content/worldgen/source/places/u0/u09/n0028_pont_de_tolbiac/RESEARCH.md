# Pont de Tolbiac

Exact OSM cadastral bridge outline way183626421 and Q2307670 identify the five-arch Seine bridge, not the demolished metal railway viaduct with a similar name. The category also contains photos of Pont de Bercy and Passerelle Simone-de-Beauvoir; those images were identified and excluded as modeling references.

AFGC gives five clear arches 29/32/35/32/29 m and 20 m useful deck with 12 m road plus 4 m sidewalks. The 1982 ministry history describes round cutwaters, flattened caps and narrow pilasters. Séjourné, *Grandes voûtes* volume V, printed 84/PDF 92, gives 7.09 m rise for the 29 m outer arch, 8.18 m for the 35 m central arch, and 17 mm/m road slope. The PDF page and table crop were inspected. The commonly published 168 m nominal length does not agree with mapped pier spacings combined with the published clear spans. The model preserves the spans and map-fitted pier widths 3.4/4.4/4.4/3.4 m, producing a 172.6 m vault chain and 200.17 m overall wings. This discrepancy remains explicit.

Own-work photos by Chabe01 dated6June2026 show the road centerline and wide broken edge stripes, paved sidewalks, pierced stone parapet and tall ornamental lamps. Close photographer references show each balustrade opening has a vertical capsule form, molded edges, dentil cornice, stone-block soffits and coursed pier caps. These are geometry and shared surface detail, with no photographs embedded.

The curved quay approaches include staggered ashlar joints, cornices, parapets, endposts and drains. Two Lorraine memorial plaques are modeled side by side on the solid first downstream panel of the left bank. The 1952 ceremony photograph reproduced by the Fondation de la France Libre (PDF page 10, printed 90) confirms this backing and position; its location inventory (PDF page 53, printed 133) and the Musée de la Résistance identify the replacement plaques. Close photographs support the inscriptions, fasteners and winged Lorraine badge. Across the road, the bridge name and 1879–1882 dates have the observed concave-corner double molding. Lettering is original geometry from the Gentilis typeface under SIL OFL 1.1; the complete license is in FONT-LICENSE.txt. Quay stairs beyond the recorded bridge footprint are outside this asset.

The footprint origin is [2.381000162446853, 48.83290412880705], with heading 0.695619343418 radians: local +X faces northeast toward Bercy and +Z southeast/upstream. The aerial export was inspected and its geographic extent retained. Map-derived geometry: © OpenStreetMap contributors, ODbL-1.0.

Modern vertical placement uses the official IGN upper-road ground samples and BD TOPO 3D road axes. The asset origin is 27.4 m in NGF-IGN69; modeled road endpoints are approximately 35.45/35.54 m, matching upper-bank samples around 35.4/35.6 m. The 3 m terrain grid interpolates across abrupt quay edges, so its edge-ray residuals are documented separately in geographic QA. The 28.5 m model reference water differs from the sampled 28.05 m RGE water surface: river level is variable and is not used as the sole structural datum. Durable raw requests, responses and hashes are in content/earth/structures/evidence/bridge-terrain/ign/N0028-bank-profile.json.
