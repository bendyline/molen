# Leshan Giant Buddha review

The 71-metre seated figure has a distinct face, ears, curled hair, hands on the knees and feet at the river terrace. The nine-turn stair follows the mapped path on the north cliff. The carved details, bedding strata and stair grades are photographic approximations. [Overview](preview.png)

Six GLBs passed glTF validation with zero errors, warnings or embedded images. Skyline and district total 312,912 bytes. [QA record](qa.json)

The map point identifies the statue, and the measured platform railing sets its river-facing bearing. The cutout overlaps the native shoreline opening to clear the coarse DEM at the rock face. Only the cliff and terrace are replaced. The water and surrounding temple buildings remain host features. Ground contact uses the available Terrarium samples.
