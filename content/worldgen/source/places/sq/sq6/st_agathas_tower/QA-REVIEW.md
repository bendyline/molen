# St. Agatha’s Tower review

The four red turret crowns, recessed arched doorway, stone stair and timber bridge render on the mapped southeast approach. The source deck stays over an open gap in all four viewer tiers.

The viewer replaces one tower footprint and the matching procedural footbridge. It retains the neighboring building and six other transportation records. The foundation extends 65 cm below the tower datum into the sampled terrain. The first step stands about 16 cm above the approach in the district, street and close-up models. A failed load restores the native tower and bridge, and unload releases the model.

The photographs guide the stair and bridge proportions. The host terrain guides the fitted base and path join. The surrounding entrenchment, interiors and adjacent mast remain outside the tower model. [Red Tower visitor guide](https://redtowermalta.wordpress.com/a-brief-tour/)
