# Jamuna Bridge — engineering evidence and current state

The model represents the original1998 concrete road crossing. The northern steel railway bridge opened in2025 is a separate structure.

## Measurements

[IABSE2005 engineering proceedings](https://www.iabse-bd.org/old/proceedings2005RP1.pdf), paper printed pp2–5 (PDFpp8–11), supplies47×99.375m spans and2×64.6875m end spans: exactly4800m. Each128m approach brings the authored crossing to5056m. The owner's [project record](https://bba.gov.bd/pages/projects/6922d980dbfbab28ce04d529) independently gives4800m,49spans,50piers,18.5mwidth and128mapproaches. These resolve inconsistent rounded totals in secondary summaries.

PDFpp9–11 were rendered and visually inspected. Pier stem section is2.5×6.4m, with bearings2.1m either side of center. Pile-cap base is+11mPWD; published central road level+33.55mPWD. The later [2015 measurement paper](https://www.iabse-bd.org/session/52.pdf) figure1 gives6.2m/3.3m box depths rather than2005's6.5m/3.25m. The later profile is used and the discrepancy retained. Original segments are4m long. Approach grade is reconstructed and needs site terrain review.

## Plan and orientation

Exact [OSMway279534616](https://www.openstreetmap.org/way/279534616) carries Q10729282. Its centerline is curved and its264.98m bounding width is not a deck measurement. A smooth circle fitted to the unchanged vertices has12003.42mradius and maximum2.83m radial residual. Published structure length is used; original mapped road extends about6m beyond it at each end. The north former-railway strip and pylons, and south gas pipeline, resolve the otherwise ambiguous axis. Raw map geometry, curve fit and independent pier coordinates from [2020 testing paper](https://iabse-bd.org/2020/pdf/26.pdf) remain in map-frame.json. ## Current state

The bridge authority's [28April2026 award](https://bangla.bppa.gov.bd/upload/noa/2026-05-06-08-56-37-Signed-NOA_28.04.2026--Jamuna-Deck-Renovation.pdf) concerns conversion of the abandoned railway lane. Award is not completion evidence. The model retains a bare former railway strip and existing offset roadway divider pending current-photo verification. Proposed toll buildings and widened-road layout are not presented as completed.

[August2026 owner maintenance tender](https://www.eprocure.gov.bd/resources/common/ViewTender.jsp?TenderCancel=false&id=1319559) confirms230kV pylon poles remain. Their height, arms, insulators and service saddles are photographic reconstruction. The original structural geometry, shared-material assignment and multi-angle capture require review. Geographic elevation remains pending because local+11mPWD is not a terrain sample.
