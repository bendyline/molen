# Pyramid of Khafre

The surviving four-sided pyramid has rough limestone masonry on its lower slopes and a pale, largely intact casing above an irregular break near the summit. The four-corner footprint comes from the named map feature. Four independently generated detail levels share the Molen limestone and granite materials.
