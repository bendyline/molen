# Pyramid of Menkaure

The smallest of the main Giza pyramids has the retained lower granite courses, exposed rough limestone above and the tall scar in its north slope. The four-corner footprint comes from the named map feature. Four independently generated detail levels share the Molen limestone and granite materials. [Reference observations](reference-observations.json)
