The full editable relief records the 2014 design concept and preserves the authored terrain study. The Earth levels reuse shared concrete and painted metal. The skyline and district meshes total 203,684 bytes. Six GLBs passed validation with zero warnings and zero embedded images. [QA record](qa.json)
