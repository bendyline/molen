# Taj Mahal Palace Hotel — historic exterior; placement held

This asset represents the historic Palace wing around its open courtyard. The modern Tower, northern connector wings, neighboring buildings, Gateway of India, harbor scenery, pool, garden furnishings and navigable interiors are outside scope. Its ordinary catalog identity is `taj_mahal_palace_hotel`.

All 82 local candidate thumbnails and four complete original photographs were inspected. The 2011 northeast view controls the basalt and cream masonry, projecting windows, round corner turrets and roofline. The 2011 dome detail controls the octagonal drum, four small turrets, red ribs, open lantern and finial. The 2014 courtyard photograph establishes the open void, inward bay windows and low terrace wing. An additional frontal original and focused inspection crops resolve 12 oriel stacks, with three levels each, and the 19-bay waterfront rhythm. Three central bays are entrance vestibules; the two extreme bays wrap into the round corner turrets. Reference paths, dates, authors, licenses and byte hashes are in `reference-images.json`. No photograph pixels are model textures.

The native Protomaps/OpenStreetMap parent merges the Palace and Tower. The model retains the southern U with an explicit inferred northern interface: it extends the plane of parent vertices 13–14 to native edge 4–5. The 76 m Tower part and connector envelope are excluded. The resulting native scope is approximately 5,244 m², with a 111.82 m waterfront edge. The low courtyard terrace and upper projecting bays/turrets are photographic extensions; the native high-roof concavity remains open. This is bounded architectural interpretation, not a cadastral survey.

[Tata’s June 2024 heritage account](https://www.tata.com/newsroom/heritage/120-years-taj-mahal-photo-feature) describes a 240 ft red Florentine dome. The model interprets this as an approximate 73.152 m overall finial envelope. Its 39.5 m main eave and 52.25 m dome base are photo-directed estimates, not measured floor elevations. The generic 20 m map height conflicts with the visible building and is retained as a source tag rather than a roof control. Exact datum, crown terminology, floor levels, fine stone carving, tracery and unseen roof details remain uncertain.

All four authored levels retain the historic U, open courtyard, main red dome and lantern, four corner crowns and waterfront oriel rhythm. The two nearer levels carry more cream framing, balcony rails, ribs and stone detail. District rear windows and skyline façades use sparse opaque proxies. Model-specific checks exercise actual imported meshes and source geometry, including the open lantern, projected bays and gaps, deep backed arcades, native wall edges, low courtyard terrace, missing-Tower probes and sampled roofs. These checks do not establish a global geometric error bound.

Geographic completion is held. The independently chosen dry pavement datum and native scope were fixed before original Terrarium tiles were sampled and before geometry was created. All 66,049 review values can be reproduced from those retained tiles with `terrain-sources/verify-derived-grid.py`. The unchanged urban/coastal surface field varies by roughly −17.19 to +2.85 m along the native perimeter, mixing building, land and coastal elevation artifacts. It cannot support valid ground contact. The fixed architectural basement foot and threshold are −0.65 and +0.15 m; they have not been stretched to fit this field. No terrain flattening, cutout, generic parent-footprint replacement or active Earth placement has been added. The linked story is not marked complete for this model.

Current source, runtime, input and capture identities are bound in `qa.json`. Exterior material and lighting approval is separate from failed geographic contact. The model remains in the local 100 until placement can pass against compatible ground; the overall goal remains active.
