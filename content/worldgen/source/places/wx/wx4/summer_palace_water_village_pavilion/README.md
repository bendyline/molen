# Summer Palace · Water Village lakeside pavilion

The 2020 photograph shows four round timber posts, a square stone platform, a low hipped roof and a stepped finial beside the pond. The street and close-up models add the upper lattice, three bench rails and the stone approach step. The eave dimensions and rear joinery are estimated. All four detail levels reuse the shared slate, ashlar and timber graphs. [Photographed walk](https://cj.sina.com.cn/articles/view/7263825367/1b0f52dd700100qjrz?tj=none&tr=9) · [Pavilion inventory](https://blog.sina.com.cn/s/blog_bdbef0ce0102wege.html)

The aerial candidate platform lies inside mapped water, about 16 m from the nearest mapped path. The photograph shows a dry flagstone approach. The model is available for local review while the platform and bearing remain unverified. [Reference observations](reference-observations.json)
