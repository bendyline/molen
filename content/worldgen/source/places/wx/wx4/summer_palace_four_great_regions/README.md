# Summer Palace · Four Great Regions

The Sumeru hall and lower court anchor the restoration plan on the mapped slope. Fourteen surrounding hall forms and four colored stupas retain the plan relationships. Six footprints use mapped buildings. Other shrine dimensions, terrace levels and roof ornament are reconstructions from the published plan, photographs and sampled terrain.

Rebuild this model with `node packages/worldgen/scripts/generate-summer-palace.mjs --ids=summer_palace_four_great_regions`.
