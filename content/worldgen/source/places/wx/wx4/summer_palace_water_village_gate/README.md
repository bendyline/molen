# Summer Palace · Water Village gate and fence

The Water Village gate has a charcoal-tiled canopy, shallow stone feet and diamond-lattice fences. Both posts carry red lanterns in the park’s July 2026 photograph. The lanterns in the model represent that photographed display. [2017 photograph](https://g.udn.com.tw/upfiles/B_GE/ger0830/PSN_PHOTO/631/f_21127631_1.jpg) · [July 2026 festival photographs](https://www.summerpalace.net.cn/noticelist/detail/1000685.html)

The World Viewer places the gate across the paved approach to the south range, about 2.2 m west of the range midpoint. The gate faces the approach shown in both ground photographs.
