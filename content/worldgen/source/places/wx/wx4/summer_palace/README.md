# Summer Palace

The world viewer can stream 43 Summer Palace sites independently. Each model has four detail levels and its own geographic anchor and terrain fit. Use `node packages/worldgen/scripts/generate-summer-palace.mjs` to rebuild the models from source.

The source uses shared ashlar, marble, timber, tile, lime plaster, glazing and painted metal surfaces. The deck follows the 134 m mapped bridge centerline and meets each path within 2 cm in the test terrain. The Long Corridor uses the mapped gallery path near the Inviting Moon Gate. The audience hall faces east and the three theater stages open toward the northern spectator court. The pavilion uses the native shore path and bridges. Painted motifs, carved figures and interior ornament are simplified.

The market canal uses a local water surface and stone banks where the standard terrain would submerge the shopfronts. The Garden of Harmonious Pleasures uses the native pond with a local terrain correction. The Bridge of Knowing the Fish has seven beam openings. Both footbridges follow their PMTiles paths. Suzhou Street covers the mapped central market reaches, with approximate shop counts beyond those reaches.

Each of the six West Dyke crossings follows its own mapped span. The Jade Belt Bridge includes a reconstructed south landing to meet the mapped dyke path. Jingming Pavilion uses the mapped lake platform and its three roof ranges are reconstructed from photographs. The eastern residences use distinct courtyard footprints for the halls of Jade Ripples and Joyful Longevity and for Yiyun Hall.

Wenchang Tower's masonry vault keeps the mapped footpath open below its green-roofed pavilion. The Bronze Ox sits on its tiered marble pedestal on the east shore and looks out over Kunming Lake. The Hall for Listening to Orioles follows six mapped building footprints, including the two-storey stage and upper terrace. Its covered links and stair profiles are reconstructed from the photographs and sampled terrain.

The Four Great Regions use the mapped Sumeru hall and lower court to place the gold-roofed halls, red terraces and four colored stupas. The Duobao Glazed Pagoda has its own eastern-slope placement, with three octagonal drums, seven eaves and seated glazed figures at close range. The north-slope models fit the published restoration plan to standard terrain. Smaller shrine dimensions and decorative reliefs are approximate. [Restoration plan and photos](https://xinwen.bjd.com.cn/content/s62282db3e4b02e911726bea0.html) · [Combined viewer fit](north-slope-fit-report.json)

The lower Sumeru entrance includes the seven-roof Cifu Archway, the restored Baohua and Fazang towers, and the open hall platform with 54 stone column pads. Its close-up stairs meet the Four Great Regions stairs within 16 cm at the two sampled joins. The combined viewer scene retains the mapped pedestrian route from Suzhou Street and gives each of the 17 replaced footprints one owner. Archway alignment and stair grades are approximate. [Restoration photos](https://gygl.beijing.gov.cn/mlgy/mlgy_gyjg01/202203/t20220309_2626215.html) · [Combined approach review](sumeru-approach-fit-report.json)

Xiuyi Bridge follows the 31 m footpath crossing at Kunming Lake’s southern outlet. The stone arch clears the native water and both deck ends meet the mapped paths within 6 mm in the viewer test. Purple Cloud Gate Tower follows the mapped hill path and preserves at least 3.17 m of clearance at the three sampled points across the vault. [Xiuyi Bridge review](../summer_palace_xiuyi_bridge/shared-capture-report.json) · [Purple Cloud Gate Tower review](../summer_palace_purple_cloud_gate/shared-capture-report.json)

The East Palace Gate includes its five-bay roof, open studded doors and modeled bronze lions. The North Palace Gate includes a seven-bay upper balcony, three ground-level passages and two flanking ranges on their mapped footprints. The viewer checks the three open passages and the mapped approach through each gate at every detail level. Door positions, individual stair heights and the carved ornament are approximate. [East Palace Gate review](../summer_palace_east_palace_gate/shared-capture-report.json) · [North Palace Gate review](../summer_palace_north_palace_gate/shared-capture-report.json)

Wenchang Gallery follows the museum's mapped courtyard ring and exhibition halls. The western gateway and north-facing memorial hall of Yelü Chucai Shrine stand in their own precinct. Wenchang Tower, the gallery and the shrine render together, replacing twelve separate footprints and retaining the native lakeshore path. The placement of the gates within the precincts and the positions of small ornaments are approximate. [Combined viewer review](eastern-courts-fit-report.json)

The Bronze Pavilion uses cast-bronze materials and a tiered marble podium in the Five-Direction court. The Revolving Archive includes two flanking sutra pavilions and the mapped Kunming Lake stele. The archive now owns the three archive footprints formerly included in the Tower of Buddhist Incense model. The terrace and stair layouts are fitted to the standard hill terrain and remain approximate. [Combined south-slope fit](south-slope-fit-report.json)

New Palace Gate has a grey-tiled gatehouse, stone side wings and an open central passage aligned with the mapped east-dyke approach. The gate center and facade bearing are approximate. Strolling Through a Picture Scroll uses four mapped building footprints for Chenghui Pavilion, both flanking towers and the upper hall. Covered stairs connect the representative terrace levels. Smaller pavilions and rockery remain outside the modeled scope. [New Palace Gate viewer review](../summer_palace_new_palace_gate/shared-capture-report.json) · [Picture Scroll viewer review](../summer_palace_picture_scroll/shared-capture-report.json)

The Garden of Farming and Weaving includes the Water Training School courts, a long covered gallery, waterfront pavilions and three northern ranges on 14 mapped footprints. It keeps the native canal, ponds and paths. The courtyard paving sits above the native landcover surface. The photo and mapped footprints guide the exterior reconstruction. The identities of smaller outer structures and the stele remain open. [Garden viewer review](../summer_palace_farming_garden/shared-capture-report.json)

The Silkworm Deity Temple stands west of the school with its closed red gates, grey stone arch, tiled enclosure and weathered limestone rockery. Its modeled path meets the native shore path at all four detail levels. The courtyard outlines and rock placements are approximate. [Visitor photos](https://note.com/onosan_at_china/n/nf912b26c3d73) · [Temple viewer review](../summer_palace_silkworm_temple/shared-capture-report.json)

The Farming and Weaving Stele model includes the rounded slab, three large engraved characters, two planter recesses and the four-post shelter. Visual review covered the source and all four detail levels. Its geographic entry remains a draft in the northern garden near the west wall. The exact shelter location and ground fit still need confirmation. [Photo references](https://www.sohu.com/a/754388313_121124386) · [Draft model review](../summer_palace_farming_stele/shared-capture-report.json)

North Ruyi Gate has open lacquered doors under a flat tiled cap and a smaller side passage. South Ruyi Gate has buff brick piers, a curved tiled crown and four gold rosettes. The models preserve their mapped pedestrian approaches, with at least 3.38 m and 3.03 m of clearance at the measured openings, respectively. South Ruyi Gate sits 4.6 m along the mapped path toward the park from the estimated entrance point to keep its masonry on the canal bank. Both exact facade lines remain approximate. [North Ruyi Gate viewer review](../summer_palace_north_ruyi_gate/shared-capture-report.json) · [South Ruyi Gate viewer review](../summer_palace_south_ruyi_gate/shared-capture-report.json)

The modern West Gate includes its grey hip-and-gable roof, bronze guardian lions, checkpoint shelter, ticket offices and 37.7 m canal crossing. In the standard viewer terrain, the bridge deck meets both mapped paths within 1.4 cm and the central checkpoint passage has 2.73 m of measured clearance. The gate alignment, bridge section and carved details are approximate.
