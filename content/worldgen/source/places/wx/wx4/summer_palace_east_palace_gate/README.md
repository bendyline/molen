# Summer Palace · East Palace Gate

The East Palace Gate is fitted to the occupied footprint on the approach to the Hall of Benevolence and Longevity. The three passages show the door leaves open. Bronze lions and the central stair relief are interpreted from photographs. Separate service ranges in the forecourt remain map geometry.

Rebuild this model with `node packages/worldgen/scripts/generate-summer-palace.mjs --ids=summer_palace_east_palace_gate`.
