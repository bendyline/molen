# Summer Palace · South Ruyi Gate

The photographed grey and buff masonry gate has a curved tiled cap, four rosettes and an unobstructed pedestrian opening. The gate center and bearing are estimated along the mapped canal-side footpath, 4.6 m inside the shoreline corner to keep the piers and wing on the bank. Plaque lettering and small roof figures are simplified. The neighboring canal, approach paths and Xiuyi Bridge are separate features.
