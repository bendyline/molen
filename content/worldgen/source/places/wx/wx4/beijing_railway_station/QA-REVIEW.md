# Beijing Railway Station

The Beijing Railway Station model includes the two clock towers and three recessed entrance bays, both open courtyards, eight rows of platform roofs, and the elevated passenger connector with supported stairs. The plaza and entrance orientation follow the mapped frontage and ordinary station photographs. [Beijing station entrance photograph](https://zdzqgw.beijing.gov.cn/zqfw/bjzdq/bjzdt/202405/t20240506_3661894.html)

All four levels meet the main threshold within 5.2 cm at three sampled points. Street and close-up meet the northern pavement within 2.3 cm at ten sampled points. The distant levels simplify the apron edge. The fixture measures at least 4.24 m below the platform canopy skin at the sampled points across all eight rows. All 207 transportation records and 87 neighboring building records remain. Failed loads retain the original station, while unloading restores the original ground and releases model resources.

Forecourt and platform elevations are visual estimates from the mapped plan and standard terrain. The platform roofs use locally fitted support heights and retain the eight modeled rows and existing rail gaps. The model depicts the permanent passenger exterior. Fine thresholds, hidden rooms and temporary works are approximate or outside the model scope.
