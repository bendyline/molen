# Summer Palace · Garden of Farming and Weaving

Fourteen occupied PMTiles footprints establish the Water Training School courts, the inland gallery, Yanshang Studio, Jade River Studio and the northern court ranges. Exterior photographs guide the grey tiled school façades, timber screens, light door and stone paving. Individual heights, courtyard paving alignments and the functions of unlabelled ranges are reconstructed. The native shoreline, ponds and public paths are retained.
