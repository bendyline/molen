# Summer Palace · Garden of Farming and Weaving

Fourteen occupied PMTiles footprints establish the Water Training School courts, the long gallery, the waterfront pavilions and northern court ranges. Exterior photographs guide the grey tiled school façades, timber screens, light door and stone paving. Individual heights, courtyard paving alignments and the functions of unlabelled ranges are reconstructed. The native shoreline, ponds and public paths are retained. Outer landscape structures and the stele await identification.
