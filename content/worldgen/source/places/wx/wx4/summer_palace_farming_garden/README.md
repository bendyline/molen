# Summer Palace · Garden of Farming and Weaving

Fourteen occupied PMTiles footprints establish the Water Training School courts, the inland gallery, Yanshang Studio, Jade River Studio and the northern court ranges. Exterior photographs guide the grey tiled school façades, timber screens, light door and stone paving. Individual heights, courtyard paving alignments and the functions of unlabelled ranges are reconstructed. The native shoreline, ponds and public paths are retained.

The walking map locates Water Village north of the school. The model places three ranges on PMTiles features `35184416066642`, `35184416066643` and `35184416066644`. The July 2026 park photograph shows the tea-room facade at the end of the paved approach through the gate. The aerial images place that approach at the south range, `35184416066642`. The recessed veranda, timber posts, glazed doors and low stone infill follow the photographed facade. [Recent festival photograph](https://www.summerpalace.net.cn/noticelist/detail/1000685.html) · [Beijing TV facade photograph](https://www.thepaper.cn/newsDetail_forward_8235946) · [Placed preview](site-preview.png)

The two rear ranges have long tiled ridges over recessed masonry gables. Their size and alignment follow PMTiles and the visible roofs in the winter aerial. A 2003 photograph from Water Village shows the local masonry detail. Individual room uses and obscured facade details are unverified. [Winter aerial](https://wayback.maptiles.arcgis.com/arcgis/rest/services/World_Imagery/WMTS/1.0.0/default028mm/MapServer/tile/11475/19/198491/431459) · [Field photograph on page 34](https://nodai.repo.nii.ac.jp/record/261/files/KJ00004317765.pdf#page=8)

A 2020 visitor photograph shows a four-post pavilion with a grey hip roof and a flagstone approach. The source-built pavilion has four detail levels. The candidate platform lies inside mapped water `3145780`, about 16 m from the nearest mapped footpath. The photograph shows dry land, and the pavilion’s exact location remains open. A separate photo survey identifies one additional pavilion inside the Water Training School and one west of Jade River Studio.
