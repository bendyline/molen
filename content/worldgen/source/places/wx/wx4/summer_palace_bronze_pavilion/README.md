# Summer Palace · Bronze Pavilion and Five-Direction court

The bronze hall follows the west-slope footprint and the documented 7.55 m height. The green-roofed Five-Direction ranges and marble stair courts are interpreted from photographs and fitted to standard terrain. Ornament and copper lattice are modeled at exterior scale.

Rebuild this model with `node packages/worldgen/scripts/generate-summer-palace.mjs --ids=summer_palace_bronze_pavilion`.
