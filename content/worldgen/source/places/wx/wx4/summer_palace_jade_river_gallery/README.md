# Jade River Gallery

Two roofed spans flank Yanshang Studio. The western span starts at the small, open pavilion. The eastern span meets Jade River Studio. Both larger studios are already drawn by the Garden of Farming and Weaving model.

The roof alignments use dated aerial imagery tied to the two studio footprints in Protomaps. The north-bank walk is dry against the sampled PMTiles shore. The exhibit photographs show dark green square posts, red lattice above the openings, painted crossbeams, a low seat rail and timber-framed reliefs in a plaster wall over gray masonry. Ceramic roof, ashlar, brick, plaster and painted wood use five shared Molen material graphs. The trim patterns and relief compositions are simplified.

The covered route around the east bank and remaining courtyard buildings require further identification. No current PMTiles feature represents this gallery separately.

Public references and observations are listed in `reference-observations.json`. Rebuild the master with `node models/generate-jade-river-gallery.mjs` from this directory. The authored levels in `spec.json` build through the standard landmark LOD generator.
