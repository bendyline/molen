# Summer Palace · Sumeru Lingjing lower court and Cifu Archway

The intact four-post Cifu Archway and two restored side towers use the lower north-slope court axis and its mapped side ranges. The main hall is represented by its current roofless display platform with 54 column pads. Two coarse building proxies inside the documented hall terrace are replaced by its low masonry. The staircase to the display platform joins the two adjacent Four Great Regions stair endpoints. Platform proportions, fine ornament and individual footing positions are approximate.

Rebuild this model with `node packages/worldgen/scripts/generate-summer-palace.mjs --ids=summer_palace_sumeru_entrance`.
