# Summer Palace · Chengxian covered gallery

The covered walk turns south from Jade River Studio toward the Hall of Freshness and Clarity. The model includes a continuous ceramic roof, open walkway, painted beams, and a timber rail on the pond side. The alignment follows the park plan and photographs, fitted between the two existing buildings with clearance from the PMTiles shore. Roof dimensions, post spacing and the covered joins are approximate under the trees.
