# Summer Palace · Hall of Jade Ripples

The hall, wing buildings and open courts follow the occupied roof footprints. Painted brackets, latticework and courtyard ornaments are interpreted at exterior scale. The east and west chambers belong to this residence. Their occupied footprints are excluded from the Hall of Benevolence and Longevity placement.

Rebuild this model with `node packages/worldgen/scripts/generate-summer-palace.mjs --ids=summer_palace_jade_ripples`.
