# Summer Palace · Water Village Stone Bridge

The beam bridge crosses the narrow water arm on the south approach to Water Village. The model follows the photographed three rectangular openings, ashlar piers, low stone parapets and scroll details. The mapped footpath supplies the 14.4 m span and alignment. The viewer uses its native pond surface and the sampled approaches. [Bridge photograph](https://www.china-qiao.com/ql01/bjql378.htm) · [Fit evidence](reference-observations.json)

The four detail levels use shared ashlar and granite. The exact width, relief depths and stone courses are estimated.
