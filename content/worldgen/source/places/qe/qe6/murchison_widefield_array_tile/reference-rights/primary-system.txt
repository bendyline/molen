Primary 2013 MWA system authors, sections2.3/2.4: 128 tiles and analog beamformers. Each complete screen is5x5m, assembled from three steel mesh sheets; wire interval50mm and thickness3.15mm. Screen rests directly on ground, cardinal orientation within2degrees. Each tile has16 dual-polarisation antennas in4x4 pattern at1.10m spacing, two orthogonal vertical aluminium bat-wing dipoles per antenna. Cables lead to an intrinsic analog beamformer. The complete system also has16 independent weather-tight RF-shielded receiver enclosures with cooling, and shared CPF; these are separate site coverage, not part of this tile geometry.
