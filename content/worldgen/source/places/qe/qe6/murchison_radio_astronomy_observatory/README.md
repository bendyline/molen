# Murchison Radio-astronomy Observatory

An approximate late-2013 observatory ensemble with 25 independently approved assets and 202 placed instances: 36 ASKAP antennas, 128 original MWA tiles, 16 MWA receivers, the original EDGES fourpoint instrument, the power/data distribution hub, eighteen support buildings, a water tank and a separate equipment base. Identical equipment reuses geometry; each instance has a distinct placement. There is no aggregate GLB or duplicate observatory-wide placement.

The original numerical inventory contains twenty building ways, all covered by complete outside bodies, and one later SKA-Low relation classified as external to the 2013 ensemble. `route-coverage.json` records 203 bounded identity membership tests, including all original equipment points and explicit receiver candidates. These are set-coverage checks, not geometry rays or a survey of the current property. Later MWA configurations, SKA-Low development, landscaping, buried services and interiors are excluded from this historical scope.

ASKAP and original MWA tile coordinates retain their numerical source conventions, including southwest corners for the MWA tiles. The sixteen receiver identities and their eight-tile connections come from the original 2013 operator configuration. Receiver locations are explicitly inferred author choices from conditional range constraints; they are not operator receiver coordinates or measured error bounds. External receiver dimensions and finish remain estimates. EDGES registration, distribution-hub position, unlabeled support-building functions and retrospective 2013 appearance retain their individual uncertainties. The separate mapped equipment base has unverified upper apparatus and historical survival; no upper antenna is invented or claimed absent.
