# Kievyan Bridge — reference evidence

City tourism inventory gives364m length,26m width,58.3m height and1956 completion. The city's2024 repairs page instead gives351m length and60m height. Both are retained, and neither silently replaces the272.304m exposed map outline. Internal spans and cross-sections remain photographic reconstructions.

Primary photographs show two broad arch vaults separated by an open central slit, rectangular open-spandrel columns, basalt-faced transverse portal piers, longitudinal deck girders, and smaller bank access arches. Own-work photographs were viewed at their Wikimedia Commons file pages; no photography is embedded as model texture.

The city's9July2026 repair article and its7July2026 video were reviewed in-browser. Playback frames at5,15,25,35,45,55,65,75,90seconds were inspected; earlier seeking attempts did not advance decoded frames and are not treated as evidence. The footage clearly resolves overlapping circular cast-iron rings, open eight-point rosette centers and raised circular beads, fluted rail posts, existing branched lamp poles and deck joint construction. Temporary equipment and removed paving are not permanent architectural geometry. The announcedAugust reopening is not evidence that all repairs finished.

A1955 architecture book was searched but did not provide a Kievyan plan. JICA2012 inventory identifies this crossing asHRA-2; the main report points to a separate detailed data book without supplying dimensions here. These sources do not establish115m as an engineering dimension. Model span115m remains an explicit reconstruction needing further confirmation.
