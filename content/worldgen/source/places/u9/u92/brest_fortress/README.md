# Brest Fortress

Brest Hero Fortress includes the Star entrance, Kholm and Terespol gates, Saint Nicholas Garrison Church, surviving brick barracks and White Palace ruins, and the Courage, Thirst and Bayonet memorials.

The three gates retain their open passages and the mapped approaches. The site uses original geometry and shared material graphs.

Run the Brest Fortress generator registered in `asset-build.json` to rebuild the detailed source. The four levels share brick, limestone, plaster, concrete, granite and three metal finishes. Sculptures and ruin profiles interpret the reference photos. The site model covers the central citadel and main memorial ensemble.
