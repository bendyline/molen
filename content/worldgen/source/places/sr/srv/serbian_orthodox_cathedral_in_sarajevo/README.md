# Serbian Orthodox Cathedral in Sarajevo

![Molen preview](preview.png)

Individual Sarajevo Orthodox Cathedral: rose-brown lime-plaster walls with creamy pilasters, round-headed recessed windows/portals and shallow ornamental cornices. West projecting square entrance/bell tower has tall louver openings, clocks and pediments, dark sheet-metal swelling Baroque cap, upper louvered lantern/onion and gold cross. Broad central windowed drum/dome and four corner windowed drums/domes rise over a dark hipped roof and three eastern rounded apses. Curved shoulder gables, simple grille glazing, entrance landing/stairs and shared plaster/stone/metal/wood surfaces. Four browser levels preserve the single tall bell tower plus five domes. Fine iconography/sculpture, interiors and surveyed urban ground/threshold joins are simplified or excluded.
