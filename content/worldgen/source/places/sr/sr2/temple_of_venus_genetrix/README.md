# Temple of Venus Genetrix

![Molen preview](preview.png)

Specific surviving Venus Genetrix remains: three mapped re-erected fluted Corinthian columns in a straight southwest-side row, layered bases, two leafy capital rows and volutes, concave abaci, straight three-fascia architrave, original simplified scrolling frieze, bead/dentil rhythms and the conspicuously broken three-mass cornice. Approximate warm weathering/white repairs and metal shaft hoops; mapped low cella/podium fragments with an open interior. No intact temple roof, pediment or full colonnade is restored; surrounding forum monuments stay independent.
