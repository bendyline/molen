# Apostolic Palace

The model reconstructs the historic palace ranges, the glazed San Damaso loggias, surrounding courtyards and the exterior of the Sistine Chapel. The Vatican Museums, St Peter’s Basilica and the colonnades remain separate mapped features. The narrow palace yards and lower walls follow the available standard terrain. Interior rooms and frescoes are outside the exterior model.

Rebuild the master with `node packages/worldgen/scripts/generate-apostolic-palace.mjs`. The model uses five existing shared materials for brick, travertine, terracotta, copper and glazing.
