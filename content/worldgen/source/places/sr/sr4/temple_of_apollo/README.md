# Temple of Apollo, Pompeii

![Molen preview](preview.png)

Specific standing Apollo sanctuary at Pompeii: roofless raised podium/cella with two tall fluted front columns and varied broken stumps, frontal pale/brick stair, separate pale altar with rolled ends and sundial column; surrounding open court has surviving/broken fluted portico shafts and a short east entablature, rough enclosing walls and an original simplified volumetric bronze replica figure. No restored temple roof/pediment, broad precinct slab or copied sculpture/text atlas.
