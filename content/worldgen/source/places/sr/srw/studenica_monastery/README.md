# Studenica Monastery

The Church of the Virgin has an ochre west narthex, pale marble nave and red octagonal drum under a ribbed grey dome. The smaller King’s Church and Saint Nicholas stand inside the sloping enclosure between the west gate tower and the paired round towers of the east gate.
