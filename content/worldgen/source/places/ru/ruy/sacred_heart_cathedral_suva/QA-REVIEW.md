# Sacred Heart Cathedral, Suva review

Sandstone towers with pierced crowns and louvered belfries flank the upper loggia. Both portals have genuine recesses and the east nave follows the surveyed walls. [Overview](preview.png)

Six GLBs passed glTF validation with zero errors, warnings or embedded images. Skyline and district total 253,216 bytes. [QA record](qa.json)

Only the named cathedral on Pratt Street is replaced. Its entrance bearing uses the mapped west wall, and the correction clears a narrow landing while preserving the road and nearby buildings. Ground contact uses the available Terrarium samples. [Placement evidence](placement-report.json)
