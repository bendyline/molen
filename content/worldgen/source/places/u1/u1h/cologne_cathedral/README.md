# Cologne Cathedral

Cologne Cathedral’s twin Gothic spires rise over the west façade. The five-aisled nave and steep slate roof meet the two transepts and crossing spire. Double flying buttresses and radial chapels define the eastern choir. The masonry, slate, bronze trim and glazing use shared material graphs. [Reference observations](reference-observations.json)
