# Hermannsdenkmal review

The west-facing Arminius and his raised sword stand over the ten-pier sandstone arcade. Two curved eastern flights climb to a shared landing and a short upper stair. The new foundation follows the sampled slopes below the pedestal. The monument retains its 53.46 m source height. [Regional heritage record](https://www.westfalen-regional.de/de/hermann_externsteine/) · [Regional aerial photograph](https://www.teutoburgerwald.de/region/ausflugsziele/mein-ziel/hermannsdenkmal)

All four detail levels leave the ten bays open, meet the terrain at 16 sampled foundation points, and replace six mapped parts of the monument. They retain one independent building and all 24 transportation records in the fixture. The first treads sit about 4–5 cm above the sampled paved approaches in the three nearer models. A failed model load retains the native features.

The statue retains its published height and uses polygonal approximations of the face and clothing folds. The capital heads, waterspouts and stair profiles follow photographs and the mapped approach. Precise masonry, carving and riser dimensions are approximate.
