# Afsluitdijk

Reviewed 2026-11-18. The model is ready in the World Viewer catalog.

The 31.729 km model includes the raised causeway, carriageway and cycle path, five historic sluice banks, three navigation lock chambers and the visible opening for fish passage at Kornwerderzand. The Den Oever update adds two modern sluice fronts with concrete wing walls and adjacent low pumping-station fronts at the four named PMTiles positions. Their dimensions are estimated from exterior photographs. The model depicts a static arrangement. Interiors, water motion, exact stages and future works are outside its scope. [Sluice aerial](https://www.cementonline.nl/nieuwe-spuisluizen-afsluitdijk-bij-den-oever-voor-het-eerst-geopend) · [2026 commissioning](https://www.rijkswaterstaat.nl/en/news/archive/2026/10/reinforcement-of-the-afsluitdijk-completed-pumping-station-and-new-drainage-sluices-officially-commissioned)

The model uses the named A7 corridor and its mapped end connections. At anchor `[5.225, 53.022]` the local +X axis heads toward Friesland and +Z faces the IJsselmeer. Geometry and masks share the viewer's metric Web Mercator projection. Both embankment ends taper down to the standard terrain. The two added water polygons fill the unmapped parts of the new sluice openings and meet the existing Wadden Sea and lake polygons. Their 2.15 m height matches the surrounding viewer style. Water sits below the 4.90 m decks at all six new crossing probes.

I inspected four detail levels in the production renderers at Den Oever, Kornwerderzand, midspan and the Friesland approach. Tests confirmed that removal is limited to the identified causeway surfaces, overlap with its gate-house footprints and 13 physical openings. The full archived map test preserves 573 independent building components. The visible water and original terrain samples survive loading, and tile unload restores ground, water and landcover meshes. The standard terrain has limited resolution at individual water edges. The two waterworks and staging sample distinct sections of the site.

The detailed master has 127,168 triangles. The skyline, district, street and close-up files have 944, 3,374, 13,442 and 33,168 triangles. Skyline and district total 393,836 bytes. The close-up file is 2,935,092 bytes. Near models share existing basalt, concrete, standing-seam metal, painted metal and window graphs. None of the six GLBs embeds images. All six pass Khronos validation, and four focused Afsluitdijk tests pass. Physical-device timing remains unmeasured.

The [native site preview](site-preview.png) shows the two waterworks in context. `qa.json` binds the placement, current sources, portable and shared-material previews, and 17 native-context lighting and LOD captures.
