# Forest of Remembrance

The Forest of Remembrance is a planted memorial in El Retiro. Its north causeway, curved granite channel, three timber bridges, fluted column and rising spiral follow the mapped parcel and photographs. The detailed source represents the original 170 cypresses and 22 olive trees. [Entrance panorama](https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:Bosque_del_recuerdo-panoramica.jpg) · [Madrid’s water feature inventory](https://patrimonioypaisaje.madrid.es/portales/monumenta/es/Monumentos-y-Edificios-Singulares/Monumentos/Estanque-del-Bosque-de-los-Ausentes/?vgnextfmt=default&vgnextoid=ac88091d1b9c4510091d1b9c45102e085a0aRCRD)

The three bridges meet the paths within 1.5 cm across four detail levels. The north causeway meets the native path within 1.1 cm. The viewer replaces only the authored three bridge lines, column and memorial water. It retains the neighboring park pond and restores the native geometry after unload or failed load.

136 tree positions come from map points and 56 fill the photographed planting. The summit rises approximately 5.75 m above the entrance path in the model. Terraces, current tree heights, individual species assignment and unmapped planting positions are photo-led. The plaque is represented by its dark face. Inscription text and park furniture outside the memorial parcel remain outside the modeled scope.
