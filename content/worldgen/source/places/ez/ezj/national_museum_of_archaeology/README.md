# National Archaeological Museum, Madrid

The Serrano frontage includes the two orders of columns, recessed grilles, entablature inscription, stair and paired bronze sphinxes. The same palace contains the Recoletos library entrance and sculpted pediment. The three PMTiles courtyard openings, lower inner roofs and four slate corner pavilions are modeled.

Run `node packages/worldgen/scripts/generate-madrid-archaeological-museum.mjs` to rebuild the detailed source master. The landmark LOD generator exports four levels using shared limestone, granite, brick, slate, bronze and painted metal.

OpenStreetMap via Protomaps supplies the palace plan and roof parts. Standard Terrarium elevations set the 670 m datum and the two approach grades. Stair elevations and small sculptures are exterior reconstructions from photographs.
