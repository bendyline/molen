# Guggenheim Museum Bilbao

The Guggenheim Bilbao exterior includes the curved titanium masses, limestone galleries, river atrium and canopy, plus the lowered city entrance with its stepped plaza and west-side promenade flight. The street and close-up geometry carries the museum’s documented 41 entrance steps. [Museum approach](https://www.guggenheim.org/audio/track/bilbao-approach) · [Entrance photograph](https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:Museo_Guggenheim_Bilbao-at_entrance.jpg)

All four levels replace 22 mapped museum records and retain 28 neighboring buildings and all 175 transportation records in the test tile. The modeled paving meets the native southern ground edge within 1.4 cm at the sampled approach. The test retains the river, adjacent pools and La Salve’s roadway. The original map remains visible on failed load. Unload restores the terrain and releases model geometry.

The entrance and plaza elevations, intermediate tread dimensions and west stair count are fitted from ordinary photographs and the standard terrain. Exterior accessibility hardware and exact elevation survey remain outside this reconstruction. The museum exterior retains photo-led shell profiles, gallery heights and joints. La Salve Bridge, independent artworks and museum interiors remain separate.
