# Vizcaya Bridge review

Four open lattice towers support the high truss, dipped suspension cables and stays. The suspended gondola has two passenger cabins and an open central vehicle lane. [Overview](preview.png)

Six GLBs passed glTF validation with zero errors, warnings or embedded images. Skyline and district total 424,908 bytes. [QA record](qa.json)

The model leaves 45 m of nominal clearance beneath the span away from the fixed gondola position. The PMTiles shoreline is shown at a static water elevation in the review. [Estuary preview](site-preview.webp)

All four tower footprints use mapped foundations. Only the mapped transporter line is removed from the road rendering. The two dock approaches, quays and estuary remain host features. Ground contact uses the available Terrarium samples. [Placement evidence](placement-report.json)
