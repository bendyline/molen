# Batalha Monastery

The west church façade rises above deep archivolts between paired pinnacles and flying buttresses. The Founder’s Chapel has a low octagonal lantern. Two open cloisters sit between the church and the northern ranges; the roofless Unfinished Chapels retain an open central court among their tall clustered shafts.
