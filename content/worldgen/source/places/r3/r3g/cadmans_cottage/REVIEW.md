# Cadmans Cottage review — 2026-10-17

The source exterior is reviewed; geographic placement remains held. The draft is excluded from the normal Earth preview query and has no completed-story annotation.

The two accepted Qualla front originals and the 2024 northwest rear original were inspected again. The official [2007 conservation plan](https://www.environment.nsw.gov.au/sites/default/files/cadmans-cottage-historic-site-conservation-management-plan.pdf) was downloaded and its relevant drawings, rear photograph, site plan and ground-access descriptions inspected. Its window schedule confirms 16 panes in the lower front windows, 12 above and six at the rear. The recipe now distinguishes those grids. Rear sills are approximately 0.9 m above the existing rear threshold, based on photographs; exact dimensions remain estimates.

All 38 current views were actually inspected: nine portable, eight shared-material and 21 lighting/context/detail/forced-distance images. Two added closeups expose the window changes. Five shared material graphs bind normally; glass remains local. Source regeneration, runtime import, all four LOD budgets, four schemas, six runtime pack files and a repeated 30-tick state pass. The LODs contain 374/544/1406/1984 triangles with 1/5/6/6 draws. There is no independent Khronos certificate or new ray-probe suite for this small revision. Bulk captures remain local.

The existing native frame, dry approach reference and Terrarium field remain unchanged. Two new independent candidates were examined without fitting their elevations to the model:

- The official NSW Sydney 5 m bare-earth ASC tile gives about 1.57 m of rise from the dry reference to the rear center.
- The City of Sydney's 2020 one-metre contours, triangulated from their published vertices, give about 1.53 m. These are contour intervals, not a one-metre raster. A single conflicting vertex far outside the cottage was excluded and recorded; no elevations were averaged or invented.

Both leave roughly 1.8 m below the existing 3.35 m rear threshold. They were not adopted. The plan documents retained courtyards, paving and stairs; it does not establish an absolute doorway level in the inspected material. Source interpolation and the model's depth, frame and floor estimates still need reconciliation against independent measurements. The current ground views also show lower-front burial. No pad, terrain flattening, cutout or foundation extension was introduced.

The [research record](../../../../qualla-structures/batches/research/cadmans_cottage.json) retains source URLs, original hashes, diagnostic samples, coordinate caveats and the next action. Source photographs and plan pixels are inspection references and are not redistributed as textures.
