# Parliament House, Canberra

The forecourt now follows original map water, pedestrian and planted-bed polygons,
checked against the complete official 2020 orthophoto and the local 2015 front
photograph. It has a flared outline, asymmetric basin, three water sections, three
connecting approaches and planted island beds. The previous rectangular paving and
simple circular pool were inaccurate. Independent circle fits to the aerial image
also disagreed and were rejected. The map vertices and topology are retained in
`forecourt-frame.json` and their original sources in `map-sources/`.

The [official mosaic description](https://www.aph.gov.au/Visit_Parliament/Art/Collections/Michael_Nelson_Jagamaras_Forecourt_mosaic_pavement)
gives an area of 196 m². The square is represented at 14 by 14 metres; its painting
is omitted. Basin depths, paving and planting levels remain photographic estimates.
No photograph or aerial pixels are redistributed. Main building massing, mast,
nine office/executive court voids and grass-roof grades are unchanged.

All 42 current views were inspected: 10 portable, 9 shared-material, 22 source-ground
and context views, and one isolated forecourt detail. Shared flat-ground views hide
some surfaces below their test datum; the portable and separate detail views show
the physical basin and bridges. The four actual detail levels contain
913/3927/15827/54125 triangles and pass 947 controls each. These include nine court
voids, three water patches, three bridges, three planted beds, the square plaza,
mast and 927 basin floor coverage points per level. Skyline paving is a surface
proxy; its basin floor retains the original outline. Strict detail/palette budgets,
exact regeneration/import, four schemas, six runtime pack files and repeatable
30-tick simulation pass. Bulk images remain in ignored folders.

Geographic placement remains held. The original Terrarium field is unchanged.
A new independent ACT contour candidate, sampled only after the source plan and
photographic levels were fixed, still disagrees with the flat model paving and
grass-roof contact. It was not adopted. The old reference point is outside the newly
mapped real basin, correcting the earlier diagnosis based on the obsolete proxy;
its model height still needs review. A new dry paving reference is only a proposal.

The physical basin floor, rim and island justify a bounded opening in the preview
ground. That opening changes no source elevations and is inactive in the Earth draft.
It does not resolve the broader contact issue. Official
[1981 schematic design material](https://www.aph.gov.au/-/media/05_About_Parliament/54_Parliamentary_Depts/544_Parliamentary_Library/history/PHSchematicDesignReportAug1981.pdf)
describes the garage beneath the forecourt; later
[Parliament evidence](https://www.aph.gov.au/~/media/Estimates/Live/commttee/S8317.ashx)
confirms the pond lies above the garage. These sources establish real construction,
but not verified current floor/deck levels. The current site-book download returned
403. Obtain stronger architectural section/level evidence or the original fine ground
data before changing grades or adding garage geometry. Do not extend foundations
merely to conceal a terrain mismatch. No completed-story annotation is exported yet.
