# Irpin Bridge (Hostomel)

The rebuilt M-07 river crossing has two separate concrete decks, four traffic lanes and two outer sidewalks. Blue-gray pedestrian railings, galvanized guardrails and offset light poles follow the divided roadway over the Irpin.

Run `node packages/worldgen/scripts/generate-hostomel-bridge.mjs` to rebuild the detailed master. The landmark LOD generator exports four levels. Shared board-formed concrete, deck concrete and painted steel use metric UVs.

The two carriageways and sidewalks follow four PMTiles lines, with short overlap at each native approach for a continuous join. The final deck, railings and supports use reconstruction photographs and the standard Terrarium terrain.
