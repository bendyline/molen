# New Darnytskyi Bridge

The model follows two road carriageways and two tracks across the Dnipro in Kyiv. The new bridge uses three solid steel tied arches over the central rail deck. The four detail levels share concrete and painted metal materials. The older railway and independent approaches keep their native map geometry.

Run `node packages/worldgen/scripts/generate-new-darnytskyi-bridge.mjs` to rebuild the master. The standard landmark LOD generator exports all four authored runtime levels.

OpenStreetMap via Protomaps PMTiles supplies the road and rail axes. The arch positions follow the aerial reference. Deck grades, rises and pier positions are visual estimates from standard terrain and ordinary exterior photos.
