# Temple of Hibis

![Molen preview](preview.png)

Standing Hibis temple exterior: lower roofed stone rear body with cavetto cornice, actual front/side doorways and shallow square wall sockets; higher roofless portico with varied palm/bell/bud capitals, heavy perimeter beams, low screen walls and central open doorframe. Occupied platform/short approaches and one pictured freestanding carved entrance gateway. Original simplified bands/petals/winged-disc vocabulary over shared sandstone, no copied inscriptions/relief atlas. Internal support/room inventory, other distant gates/quay, palms, precinct walls and temporary scaffold are not claimed authored.
