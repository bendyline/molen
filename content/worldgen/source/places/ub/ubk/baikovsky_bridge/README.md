# Baikovsky Bridge

The historic bridge carried Taganrog Avenue over the General Ravine in Rostov-on-Don through a long brick culvert. This reconstruction draws the shallow dip, sidewalks, rails, and lamps from a photograph of the mid-1870s. The contemporary commemorative relief informs the arch trim and wing walls. The surviving street alignment sets the bridge heading.

For a dated world-viewer host, use `viewingDate` `1876-06-01`. The 1876 date slot represents the photographed state. The viewer opens the two culvert mouths only while the historical model is resident. The surrounding modern streets, tram fixtures and buildings remain visible. The original grade, portal positions, and finish details are approximate.

Regenerate the source master with `node packages/worldgen/scripts/generate-baikovsky-bridge.mjs`. The source recipe has separate skyline, district, street, and close-up geometry with shared brick, limestone, granite, gravel, and painted metal. [Map frame and references](map-frame.json)
