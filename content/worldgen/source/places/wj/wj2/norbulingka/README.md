# Norbulingka

Norbulingka’s New Summer Palace, with saffron screens and a gilded canopy, the central palace ranges and the lake pavilion with a green hipped roof.

Ten PMTiles footprints anchor the New Summer Palace and mapped companion ranges. The island model follows the native lake and connects to the bank.

Run the Norbulingka generator registered in asset-build.json to rebuild the detailed source. The four detail levels reuse lime plaster, ashlar, timber, clay roof tile, patinated metal, gilt metal and canvas. Carving and roof ornaments are interpreted at World Viewer scale.
