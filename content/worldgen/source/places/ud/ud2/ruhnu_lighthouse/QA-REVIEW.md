# Ruhnu Lighthouse review

Ruhnu’s riveted center shaft, four inclined legs, two tiers of braces and lantern render on the hilltop. The model points toward the mapped approach and uses the center of the circular shaft footprint. The mapped center is about 2.3 m south of the navigation marker, and the entrance points along the final approach.

Across all four levels, the tops of the granite pads sit about 9–14 cm above the sampled terrain. Two horizontal traces remain open between the shaft and legs. The viewer replaces the mapped shaft extrusion and keeps the four independent buildings and four transportation records in the fixture. A failed load restores the native extrusion, and unload releases the model.

The standard terrain gives the local ground fit. Plate, brace and lens dimensions are reconstructions from the restored exterior photographs. Detached station buildings remain separate. [Ruhnu Lighthouse photographs](https://visitestonia.com/en/ruhnu-lighthouse)
