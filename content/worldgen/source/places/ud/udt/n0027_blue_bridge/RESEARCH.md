# Blue Bridge — Saint Petersburg

The exact identity is Q2618925 and OSM relation3299061 over the Moika. This is not the Blue Bridge in Kronstadt. The mapped long axis follows the river, so it must not be treated as a road-crossing heading. Native+X crosses toward the southeast/Mariinsky Palace; native+Z follows the river southwest. The upstream/negativeZ face retains the ribbed cast structure and the downstream/positiveZ face is the smooth reinforced-concrete replacement.

The bridge owner Mostotrest reports29.4m overall length,95.4m width at cornices and113.85m at wing walls. The contractor Pylon's2016 completed-projects book (PDF page23) gives15.7m span and97.3m nominal width. The page was downloaded, rendered and inspected. The designer Stroyproekt's indexed engineering reference separates57m cast and41m reinforced-concrete portions and describes the unloading concrete vault added above the cast iron during2013–2014 work. Its indexed PDF URLs currently return404; those numbers are corroborative text rather than inspected drawings.

Owner gallery photographs2,3,5,7,8,11,12,14,16 and archival images0–6 were opened and inspected. They show the two different arch faces, blue openwork Moika railing, circular frieze over concave panels with four-lobed ornaments, granite wing walls, simple inner safety railing, restored hexagonal lanterns and ornate bridge-name posts. Owner aerial photographs establish that much of the deck is a parking square. The model marks only the deck portion; surrounding road/parking and the NicholasI monument remain outside this asset.

Arch rise, water reference, casting pitch, local member dimensions and traffic markings remain reconstructions until direct survey or restoration drawings are available. They are separated from published dimensions in spec.json. The Neptune gauge on the adjacent quay is not part of this bridge mesh. Normal-water and road datum, bank contacts and exact current parking alignment require geographic review.
