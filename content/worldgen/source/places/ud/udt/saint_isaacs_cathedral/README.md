# Saint Isaac cathedral, five domes and four porticos

![Molen preview](preview.png)

Saint Isaac’s Cathedral exterior follows the mapped central body, four corner pylons and four granite-columned porticos. Recessed bronze doors on the south, west and north faces, faceted pediment reliefs, the colonnaded drum and five gilded domes distinguish its silhouette. Shared limestone, rose granite, painted metal, patinated copper and glazing supply the surfaces. Religious interiors and exact historical sculpture are outside the exterior scope.
