# Royal Palaces of Abomey

Abomey’s historic royal museum courts, red earthen reception halls, shaded verandas, polychrome reliefs and surviving circular shelters.

Twenty-five PMTiles footprints anchor the museum precinct, surviving northern halls and circular shelters. Native roads remain alongside the modeled courts.

Run the Royal Palaces of Abomey generator registered in asset-build.json to rebuild the detailed source. The four detail levels reuse earthen-tinted plaster, timber, sandstone, ashlar, corrugated metal and thatch. Relief motifs and secondary walls are interpreted at World Viewer scale.
