# Búsanyìn Shrine exterior review

A low curved step and weathered rim lead to the carved porch posts. The modeled concrete walls retain the warm ochre color and irregular openings seen in the standing-shrine photos. Both entry bays remain open and the joints between the lifted prow and corrugated wings remain covered. [Riverfront preview](riverfront-preview.webp) · [2015 photograph](https://artsandculture.google.com/asset/ZgEWgPJKkHDImg)

The master, imported GLB and four viewer levels passed Khronos validation with zero errors, warnings or embedded images. The roof coverage check crossed both porch-to-wing joins at 42 sampled points at each level. Approach probes hit the upper landing and lower tread separately. The skyline and district files total 354,468 bytes. [QA record](qa.json)

The catalog model depicts the standing exterior documented around 2015. The 2019 flood severely damaged the shrine. Its current condition and restoration remain unverified. [Conservation report](https://whc.unesco.org/document/224880)

The conservation plan and the overhead remnant fix the bank location and agree on the rear wall’s bearing within 2.3°. The porch faces the river. In the viewer, the lower tread cleared the landcover by 7 cm on the 1 m and 2.4 m trial grids, and all four model tiers loaded and released their geometry. The road, river and neighboring footprints still rendered. The reconstruction and its grade appear during 2015 and are absent from the default view.
