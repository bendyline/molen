# Osun Grove road entrance gate review

The crest shows open jaws, a forked tail and staggered scale seams. The left and right leaves carry different raised figures with folded garments, head shapes and gestures. The main opening, side apertures and emblems remain legible at approach distance. [Crest and carved lintel](crest-detail.webp)

The six GLBs passed Khronos validation with zero errors, warnings or embedded images. The first two levels total 208,956 bytes. [QA record](qa.json)

The town-facing facade matches the entrance photos and the overhead pier line. The shipped source correction uses the aerial anchor and clears 5.3 m across the gate. The road-centered fallback is 3.55 m northwest and clears 5.1 m on the retained baseline road. Both were rendered at all four levels using the owning tile’s terrain, with no host sampler. Terrain under the piers and other mapped features were retained. The short road curve under the canopy is estimated. [Signed map frame](map-frame.json) · [Placement checks](placement-report.json)
