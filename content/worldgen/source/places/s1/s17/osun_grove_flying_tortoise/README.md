# Arch of the Flying Tortoise

The arch marks a traditional approach to Ojúbọ Òṣogbo. Susanne Wenger and Adebisi Akanji created the original, and Akanji and Adebisi Nurudeen rebuilt it in 2014. This model follows photographs of the rebuilt arch from both sides. It includes the forked pedestal, the raised crest, the recessed wing dimples and the scalloped walls with a tilted opening. Shared lime plaster and weathered limestone supply the surfaces. The dimensions and adjoining wall extents are estimated. [Trust photograph and restoration record](https://artsandculture.google.com/asset/arch-of-the-flying-tortoise-susanne-wenger-and-adebisi-akanji/8gHWUfjj4HU6cA) · [Wide front photograph](https://www.ajlajournal.org/articles/culture-design-and-two-yoruba-landscapes) · [Rear photograph](https://solajegede.wordpress.com/2016/07/31/osun-osogbo-groove-home-of-goddess-of-fertility/)

The model faces southwest at about 211° true, 2 m east of survey point 32. A geotagged photograph of the shelter visible from the grove side supports the facing. The exact crossing and bearing are approximate. The model clears both mapped roads and its three supports meet native terrain. [Placement report](placement-report.json)
