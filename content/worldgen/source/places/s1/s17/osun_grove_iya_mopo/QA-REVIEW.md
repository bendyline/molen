# Ìyá Mọòpó exterior review

Three close pairs of tapered arms sweep toward the clearing, as shown in the Trust’s overhead views. All four viewer levels retain the six individual arms, upturned head and long trailing legs. Closer views show the hornbills, folded wrapper, recessed torso slit and fired-clay vessels. The apron surrounds the torso and leaves real ground visible between the trailing forms. All six GLBs passed Khronos validation with zero errors, warnings or embedded images. All four viewer levels retain the six arms and the long ground-level forms. The skyline and district files total 323,652 bytes. Three library materials resolved, and the model released its geometry on unload. [QA record](qa.json)

The 2016 survey identifies the western sculpture. The overhead film and north-up satellite imagery agree on the open side of the arm fan and the southeast trailing forms. The model’s torso is placed at the estimated fan hub, about 9 m northwest of the survey point, with local +Z facing about 225° true. The satellite streaks may include shadows. The compass bearing, outline and footing heights are approximate. The low wrapper and leg profiles follow sampled native Terrarium elevations at the approximate pose.
