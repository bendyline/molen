# Tōdai-ji Temple review

The Great Buddha Hall retains two swept tile roofs, paired gold ridge ornaments and the curved central canopy. The lower cloisters frame the open court. Nandaimon has open bays, and the hillside bell remains suspended under its own tiled roof. [Overview](preview.png)
