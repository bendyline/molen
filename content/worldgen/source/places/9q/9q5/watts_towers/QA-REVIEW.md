# Watts Towers review

The three open towers, West Tower loops, glazed court walls, gazebo, ship and house remnants retain their positions within the street-facing sculpture court. Authored street and close-up meshes preserve continuous supports and rings. The original mesh retains its detailed mosaic pieces, heart-shaped braces and nested frames. [California State Parks](https://www.parks.ca.gov/?page_id=613)

The court follows the sampled terrain at East 107th Street. All 12 interior checks per level met the model above the terrain. The skirt passed 11 of 12 edge checks in each distant level and all 12 in each near level. The viewer retained 49 neighboring building records and 13 transportation records, including on a failed load.

Sculpture positions and individual mosaic and wire details are approximate. The modern fence, amphitheater and arts center are outside this model. The narrowest rods fade at skyline distance.
