# Getty Center

The Getty hilltop includes four travertine gallery pavilions, the entrance rotunda, the circular Research Institute, the exhibition wing, auditorium and tram arrival. The modeled campus preserves the open courts and includes the Central Garden basin, azalea beds and terraces.

Run `node packages/worldgen/scripts/generate-getty-center.mjs` to rebuild the detailed master. The landmark LOD generator exports four levels. Shared travertine, concrete and painted metal use metric UVs.

OpenStreetMap via Protomaps supplies the footprints, tram arrival and garden basin. Individual heights, terrace grades, windows and roof equipment are exterior reconstructions. The lower visitor garage and tram line remain in the viewer map.
