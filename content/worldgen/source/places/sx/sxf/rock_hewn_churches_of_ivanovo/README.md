# Rock-hewn Churches of Ivanovo

Six limestone cuts above the Rusenski Lom, with the Holy Virgin’s restored masonry, wooden stairs, timber shelter and painted chambers, the Barbers’ chamber, the Archangel chapel’s multiple openings, the Baptistery’s tall recess, and the irregular niches and cavities at God’s Gap and St Theodore.

The model includes the Church of the Holy Virgin, the Barbers, Archangel Michael Chapel, the Baptistery, God’s Gap and St Theodore Church. PMTiles controls each site and approach. Local cliff patches replace the smoothed ground around the entrances while the surrounding valley uses standard terrain.

The deterministic generator listed in asset-build.json writes the master. Its four runtime detail levels retain the carved openings and use shared limestone, ashlar, timber, iron, plaster and roofing materials. The surviving frescoes informed the placement and colors of the painted bands and figures.
