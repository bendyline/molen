Factual paraphrase: whole masonry TheatreRoyal at29CampbellStreet, Hobart, has two frontage storeys on a sandstone plinth. Ground facade is rusticated sandstone; upper brick is rendered. A frieze/stringcourse divides levels. Paired pilasters and engaged Tuscan columns frame the facade, with squat pilasters above its uppercornice. A central arched entrance has a Tuscan-column portico and triangular pediment, with gilt theatre name below. Rear stage retains early stone/brick walls and has a flytower exceeding18m height. Auditorium/foyers/basement and stage belong to the original theatre. Adjacent2021Hedberg is a separate registered building, not substituted for the theatre. Mainroof profiles, exact heights/divisions/fittings/colour/opening census are not supplied and are declared estimates. No interior model or copied lettering artwork.
