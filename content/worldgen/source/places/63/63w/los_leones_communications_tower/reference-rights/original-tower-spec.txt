1.1 Introduction 
This document provides specifications for the radio tower installations for the Pierre Auger Observatory 
Engineering Array phase. The system initially requires the installation of 2 towers; 
 
1. Los Leones tower : Tower Height = 40 metres. 
 
2. Malargue Campus tower : Tower height = 50 metres. 
 
Additional towers will be added to the system as constr uction of the full Observatory progresses, it is expected 
that there will eventually be between 5 and 7 towers in the complete network. The radio system at the site 
consists of 2 main radio services; 
 
1. A ‘Cellular Type’ radio system operating at 902-928 MHz ( henceforth  called the ‘915MHz cellular 
system’ offers broad area radio coverage to an azimuth of 200 degrees centered around a bearing of  
approximately 10 degrees North. Note that all bearings quoted in this document are approximate. 
Precise bearings will be supplied before tower construction commences. 
 
2. A Microwave point to point system will operate between the Los Leones tower and the Malargue 
Campus tower. 
 
The Los Leones tower will be equipped with BOTH radio services. 
 
The Malargue Tower will be equipped to carry the microwave point to point service only. 
 
1.2 Microwave point to point system 
1.2.1 Los Leones Tower 
The Los Leones tower will initially be equipped with a single microwave antenna located at a height of 39 
metres ( to antenna centre line). This antenna will have a maximum diameter of 1.2 metres and will face towards 
the Malargue campus tower. However, a maximum of 2 additional microwave links will be added later and the 
tower should be designed to hold an additional pair of antennas mounted at the same height of 39 metres and 
directed on approximate bearings of 40 degrees North and 350 degrees North. The precise operating frequency 
and antenna size has yet to be determined. The link will carry a 34Mbps 16xE1 digital data bearer.  
1.2.2 Malargue Campus Tower 
The Malargue campus tower will initially be equipped with a single microwave antenna located at a height of 49 
metres ( to antenna centre line). This antenna will have a maximum diameter of 1.2 metres and will face towards 
the Los Leones campus tower. However, a maximum of  1 additional microwave links may be added later and 
the tower should be designed to hold an additional antennas mounted at the same height of 49 metres. 
1.3 915MHz Cellular System 
1.3.1 Los Leones Tower 
The Los Leones tower will be equipped with six ‘sector coverage’ cellular type panel antennas. A vertical 
spacial diversity scheme is to be used and the antennas are to be arranged in 2 rows of 3 antennas each. These 
panel antennas will be ‘GSM cellular panel type’ and w ill have the following approximate dimensions: height 
2.5 metres, width 0.3 metres. The first row will be located at a height of 38.5 metres ( to antenna centre line). 
The second row will be located at a height of 28.5 me tres ( to antenna centre line). Each row will have three 
antennas pointed on the following approximate bearings; 315 degrees North, 10 degrees North, 65 degrees 
North. Each antenna will have the following characteristics: Gain +17dBi, -3dB azimuth 90 degrees. The power 
handling of each antenna is very low as the system uses the 915MHz ISM band rather than actual cellular 
telephony equipment, (10 Watts maximum transmit power). All internal equipment will be installed in a single 
storey ‘communications shelter’. This building will be ap proximately 3 metres by 3 metres floor area and 2.5 
metres high to internal ceiling level. The shelter will be  made of concrete or brick and will have a either a 
pitched or sloping flat roof. The closest wall of the sh elter will be 3 metres from the closest face of the tower 
when measured at a height of 2 metres above ground level. The RF feeders will enter the building on this wall 
via a custom grounded entry panel after bending through 90 degrees from the main vertical tower run to a short

1.4 The Bid 
The bids should include the complete installation of the towers at Los Leones and Malargue including the 
associated radio equipment. Bid to include all surveys, site visits, shipping, and full construction, 
commissioning, inspection, certificati on and final acceptance of the complete d towers. The Bid should clearly 
identify any licensing  or other legal requirements placed upon th e Pierre Auger Collaboration and should also 
include a detailed description of the following : 
 
1. Tower lightning protection and grounding system including vertical lightning strip to ground rods, 
tower and shelter building ground rings and bonded grounding rods to each tower leg plus tower top 
lighting ‘spikes’ as thought appropriate. 
2. Enclosed internal safety ladder with work platforms at 10 metre intervals. 
3. The cellular panel antenna support platforms (2) , including mesh-floored work area fitted with 
handrails and kickboards. 
4. Standard microwave antenna mounts suitable for 1.2 metre parabolic antennas. 3 mounts on Los 
Leones tower and 1 mount on Malargue campus tower. 
5. The panel antennas (6), 902-928MHz +17dBi gain, 90 degree azimuth . 
6. The panel antenna RF feeders including ALL RF connectors, specified as ‘N type’ (6) . 
7. Feeder grounding kits at top and bottom of vertical feeder runs. 
8. The flexible jumper cables from antenna to feeder and from feeder to equipment rack ( inside shelter ). 
9. Individual lighting protector s (‘shorted lambda/4 stub type’) f itted to each feeder at an integral 
grounded wall feed-through panel at the shelter entry point. 
10. Feeder vertical and horizontal cable support trays and all feeder attachments. 
11. Quotations for both of the shelter buildings to be built at the same time as the towers will be welcome. 
12. Bids should include proposals for a tower maintena nce plan for each tower, including full details of 
planned frequency of visits, maintenance activities and cost schedule on an annual basis. This plan 
should be based upon a service life of 20 years.
