Introduction
 
The Pierre Auger Southern Observatory is located close to the town of 
Malargue in Mendoza Province, Argentina. The Observatory has five 
Communications Towers which provide a variety of Radio Services to the 
Experimental Equipment. This docu ment describes the location and 
technical details of all 39 transmitting antennas located at the five tower 
sites.
