# Al-Ayn Beehive Tombs review

Twenty individually mapped beehive tombs climb the rocky ridge. The weathered dry-stone shells vary in height and taper, with stepped corbelled tops, open lintelled entries and lower collapsed remains. [Overview](preview.png)

Six GLBs passed glTF validation with zero errors, warnings or embedded images. Skyline and district total 384,668 bytes. [QA record](qa.json)

Named native site geometry and local Terrarium samples establish the position and ground level. The models preserve the native ground and roads. Ground contact uses the available Terrarium samples. [Placement evidence](placement-report.json)
