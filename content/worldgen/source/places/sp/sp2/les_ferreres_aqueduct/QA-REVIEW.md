# Les Ferreres Aqueduct

Les Ferreres Aqueduct has 25 upper arches, 11 lower arches, tapered piers, and an open upper channel. The model follows the signed course and includes both entrances, the lower path, and the mapped ravine relief. The 36 open arches and channel passed 2,616 geometry probes across six GLBs. [Tarragona Turisme](https://www.tarragonaturisme.cat/en/monument/les-ferreres-aqueductpont-del-diable-bridge-mht) · [Ravine photograph](https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:Aqueducte_de_les_Ferreres.jpg)

Both upper approaches meet the channel within 13.1 cm at the sampled points. At street and close-up, 22 sampled perimeter joins are within 13.5 cm of native terrain. The coarse levels simplify the ravine and carry a wider seam tolerance. The viewer retains all 50 transportation records, the neighboring building and the drainage outside the cutout. Failed loads retain the native map. Unloading restores terrain and water and releases model geometry.

The localized ravine depth, hillside grade, scrub and exposed drainage bed are photo-led approximations between the standard terrain samples. The path network carries modeled geometric grades. Seasonal flow is unverified. The 242.225 m mapped channel includes its approaches. The tourism authority describes a 217 m surviving arcade and a maximum height of 26 m.
