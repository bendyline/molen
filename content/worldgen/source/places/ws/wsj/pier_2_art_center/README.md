A bounded, approximate historical 2015 ensemble of 32 independent geographic structures: 29 warehouse or railway-museum bodies, the container observation tower and two unlabeled ancillary buildings. Each has its own regional source, four detail levels, placement, existing accepted review and story annotation. There is no aggregate GLB or duplicate property-wide solid.

The original broad property inventory contains 38 building ways. `route-coverage.json` accounts for each once: 32 modeled bodies and six separate port or transit context features, classified in `native-map/historical-scope-reconciliation.json`. The former bank warehouse and former port workers building existed before their later coworking and hotel uses; their exclusion does not imply that those buildings were absent in 2015. The transit structures belong to a separate network opened later.

Original native plans establish horizontal envelopes. Heights, roof subdivisions, elevations, openings, finishes and ground contact retain the estimates recorded by each member. The two unlabeled ancillary buildings have unverified historical function, survival and exterior appearance; their descriptive geographic names do not assert a named warehouse identity. Current C3 and P3-1 naming does not independently establish an extra dated building. This collection is not an exhaustive measured survey of the current park.

Source grade partly buries some bases and leaves some skirts visible. `collection-review.json` binds the existing member approvals and two personally inspected current group views. These views establish assembly and coverage without another per-member approval pass. Bulk renders remain local.
