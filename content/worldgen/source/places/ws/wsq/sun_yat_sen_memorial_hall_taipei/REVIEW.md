# Sun Yat-sen Memorial Hall, Taipei

An ordinary geographically named model in the local Qualla compat batch, completed as a bounded medium-fi exterior preview. It includes the main hall, flat red roof deck, sweeping yellow tiled roofs and curled gray edges, tall south entrance, open outer colonnades, and the building’s own masonry terrace and stairs. Parks, fountain, gate piers, statues, interiors and temporary construction are excluded.

Four local originals and the official illustrated front and aerial references were inspected. Paths, attribution, hashes and inspected PDF pages are in `supplemental-reference-review.json`. The official approximate 100-metre square and 30.4-metre height are reference controls; the quantized native eaves span approximately 109 metres including curled projections. Roof curves, hidden returns, window grids and terrace details are photographic estimates, not a survey or photogrammetric reconstruction. Reference pixels are not used as textures.

All 45 current views were inspected: 11 portable, 11 shared-material/placement and 23 source-field context views. All four source and imported distance versions pass geometry attributes, triangle and draw budgets, and 113 independent checks each. These check native corners, roof coverage and hip seams, the projecting entrance, flat deck, terrace floors and clear colonnade paths. Triangle counts are 982 / 3,934 / 15,074 / 42,748; draw counts are 1 / 5 / 6 / 6. Sampled roof-height differences from close-up are at most 1.764 / 1.150 / 0.543 metres, within declared 3 / 2 / 1.1-metre budgets. These sampled checks do not establish global error bounds, structural engineering or a navigable interior.

The signed native frame and original published ground tile were frozen before geometry. A separate decoder reproduces all 66,049 interpolated values. The publisher attributes this third-party Terrarium reencoding to NLSC 2024 20-metre DTM under Taiwan’s Open Government Data License 1.0. The original official raster and private conversion pipeline were unavailable for comparison. Output pixels are about 17.3 metres apart; one-metre review samples only interpolate them and do not establish one-metre accuracy. The external dry forecourt reference is [-25,64], with provider-relative datum 7.9795 metres.

The photo-guided architectural terrace floor at 1.5 metres and foundation base at -0.4 metres preceded ground checks. Coarse raw terrain reaches 2.2585 metres under covered architecture. The native main-building polygon removes only covered ground triangles beneath the hall, terrace and entrance stairs; it does not alter source heights, add a fitted ground pad, or extend into the park or fountain. Of 745 terrace/colonnade probes, 451 fall inside this boundary; the other 294 retain original ground between -0.0204 and 1.1753 metres. The viewer restores covered ground on hiding or eviction. The portable stage separately reviews geometry, while the context fixture uses the retained source field. Acceptance applies to this bounded source preview; another DEM needs its own contact review.

`qa.json` binds acceptance to current source, runtime, input and capture hashes. `verify-geometry.mjs` checks actual source and imported geometry; `terrain-sources/verify-derived-grid.py` independently decodes the original published tile. Exact regeneration/import, asset integrity, schemas, runtime-pack inclusion and repeated 30-tick simulation pass. The linked Qualla story is annotated through the bounded completion synchronizer, whose repeat check makes zero changes. Bulk renders remain local and ignored; normal model work does not update the review lock or publish captures.
