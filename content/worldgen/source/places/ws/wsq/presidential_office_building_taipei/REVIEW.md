# Presidential Office Building, Taipei — bounded preview review

This is an ordinary geographically named catalog model. It belongs to the local Qualla compat backlog but is **complete as a bounded source preview**. Its linked story now carries the verified ready model and ordinary geographic placement.

The source uses the native main-building outline, two courtyard holes, original building parts and a signed frame fixed before geometry. All 51 local reference thumbnails and five complete local originals were inspected, including the aerial, east front, south return, courtyard and arcade details. The [official architectural description](https://english.president.gov.tw/Page/91) controls the asymmetric north facade, current flat guard/entrance roofs, differentiated arcades, four corner towers and approximately 60-metre central tower. The [official plan description](https://www.president.gov.tw/qrcode/06e) corroborates the paired courts and approximate overall dimensions.

The draft contains genuine open courts, a lower central hall, white inward courtyard walls with recesses, native cruciform tower recesses and glazing, green wing/corner roofs, rounded corner porticos, entrance stairs, and three exterior porticoed sides. The north facade is solid. Exterior proportions and hidden details remain estimates; ornate brackets, chamber details and material age are simplified to medium-fi. District and skyline versions use coarse glazing/window articulation; street preserves open portico walkways and shaft recesses with simplified balusters and reveal caps.

`verify-geometry.mjs` reproduces actual source and imported GLB checks for all four distance versions: Float32 winding/normals/UVs, geometry/draw budgets, 60 open-court probes per version, the lower central hall and tower cap, and native shaft recess/glazing and front portico air/backing checks at street and close-up. This is sampled exterior validation, not manifold/interior/navigation certification. Source regeneration/import, asset integrity, three schemas, six runtime pack files and repeat 30-tick simulation were also checked. No Khronos validator claim. Bulk current captures stay in ignored local folders.

The original composite was acquired before geometry and rejected for implausible urban relief. Its original bytes, grid, signed frame and decoder remain under `terrain-sources/rejected-composite/`. All 66,049 values still reproduce independently. Neither that field nor the model foundations were flattened or stretched.

A replacement was acquired **after geometry** from [the public tile publisher](https://github.com/ianlkl11234s/mini-taiwan-terrain). The publisher attributes its Terrarium reencoding to NLSC 2024 20-metre DTM under Taiwan Open Government Data License 1.0. The exact published PNG is retained and independently decoded. Its 17.3-metre pixels are interpolated to a one-metre review grid; this does not establish one-metre accuracy. The unchanged external dry reference [0,55] gives relative datum 5.1845 m. Original government raster and private encoding pipeline were unavailable for comparison; this is explicitly a third-party derivative. The government 2025/2019 archives returned 403. See `terrain-source-review.json` for provenance and limitations.
