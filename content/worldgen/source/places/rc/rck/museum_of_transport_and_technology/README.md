# Museum of Transport and Technology

Four independent building models represent the two MOTAT campuses in Auckland. The Aviation Hall and Sir Keith Park Station sit at Motions Road. The Western Springs Pumping Station and Te Puawānanga Science and Technology Centre sit at Great North Road.

![MOTAT buildings at both museum campuses](preview.jpg)

The viewer review uses the unchanged two-kilometre Terrarium field, with the existing member positions, headings and individually sampled ground elevations. Vertical fit remains approximate at this DEM resolution. The folded hall and railway shelter use cleared Qualla photographs. The pumping station uses its 1922 municipal illustration and heritage record. The science centre uses the mapped footprint, written building records and measured 2024 LiDAR roof profile.
