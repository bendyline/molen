# Kumsusan Palace of the Sun

The six mapped palace volumes include the rounded west gallery, stepped cornices, recessed north portico and stylized portrait panels. The north terrace has a broad stair and the two mapped fountains each retain their four separate basins. Four shared architectural material graphs use metric repeats and vertex colors.

Run `node packages/worldgen/scripts/generate-kumsusan-palace.mjs` to rebuild the detailed source master. The landmark LOD generator exports the four authored runtime levels.

OpenStreetMap via Protomaps PMTiles establishes the palace plan, tier heights and fountain outlines. Standard Terrarium samples set the 27 m placement datum and the edges of the two fountain gardens. The north apron and foundation fit within a bounded terrain cap. Site elevations, stone detailing, portraits and fountain sculpture are exterior reconstructions from published views.
