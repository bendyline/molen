# Gyeongbokgung

Gyeongbokgung’s stone-arched Gwanghwamun, axial walled courts and Geunjeongjeon throne hall, with Gyeonghoeru standing on its lake piers and Hyangwonjeong on its hexagonal island base.

Gyeongbokgung’s palace axis includes the three arched passages of Gwanghwamun, the gate courts, two principal residence halls, and the lake and island pavilions. Native terrain and ponds remain.
