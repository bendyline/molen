# Changdeokgung

Seoul’s Changdeokgung palace and Secret Garden. The double-roof throne hall, stepping terraces, three open court gates, blue-tiled council hall and residence wings lead across native paths to distinct garden pavilions above native streams and ponds.

The modeled palace courts and principal Secret Garden pavilions follow 40 mapped footprints. The gates retain their open passages and Geumcheongyo follows the mapped path across the stream. The surrounding hills, ponds and paths use native viewer data.

Run the Changdeokgung generator registered in asset-build.json to rebuild the detailed source. The four detail levels use shared gray and blue tile, timber, painted trim, granite, limestone, plaster and gravel. The exterior paint patterns and sculpture are interpreted from photographs.
