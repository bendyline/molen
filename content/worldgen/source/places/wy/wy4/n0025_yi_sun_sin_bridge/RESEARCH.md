The owner gives a2260m suspension deck,1545m main span,25.7m width and full opening on8February2013. LUSAS/Yooshin supplies357.5+1545+357.5m spans and twin steel box construction with6m deck modules. DL contractor reporting independently describes the separated aerodynamic boxes, epoxy asphalt and approximately85m maximum navigation clearance.

Gleitbau's downloaded reference sheet was rendered and visually inspected:272.7m pylon height, inclined chamfered legs and flared feet. The contractor-authored Concrete article indexed by TRB describes31m lower and21m upper clear leg separation, crossbeams at164m and255m, and2545m between cable anchors. The2012 Daelim engineering abstract gives1/9 cable sag. The2015 Alduse et al. research paper identifies29.1m aerodynamic width and3m girder depth. These describe different measured envelopes; the owner25.7m dimension is not silently replaced. Exact as-built section review remains open.

The current full-paper download requires submission of name, affiliation, email and purpose. No form was submitted, and the empty direct-file response is not treated as a downloaded drawing. Current model anchorage profiles remain photographic reconstruction.

The public owner photographs, LUSAS engineering views, Gleitbau reference sheet and aerial were inspected. Source images are not embedded in the asset or republished as textures. The source uses shared concrete and coated-metal graphs, with local asphalt and markings.

Both exact-identity OSM carriageways were retained. They cover about3795m and include approaches. The model's origin is instead registered to the midpoint of the visible tower foundations in the aerial; high tower tops are visibly displaced by parallax and are not ground control points.
