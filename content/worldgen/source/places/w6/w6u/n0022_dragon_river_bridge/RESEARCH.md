# Dragon Bridge — exterior evidence

The model follows the **three-arch, five-tube** system described by its designers. The tube groups remain visibly separate and pass through cut horseshoe connector plates; pairs of splayed hangers contain three rods apiece.

- [WSP project](https://www.wsp.com/en-us/projects/fire-breathing-dragon-bridge-vietnam): designer account, total length, six lanes and sidewalk provision.
- [ASME interview](https://www.asme.org/topics-resources/content/dragon-bridge-breathes-fire-into-economy): primary interviews with the design engineers; close photographs of the connector, hangers, dorsal scales and east-facing head were inspected.
- [Engineering paper](https://doi.org/10.1051/e3sconf/20161000106): pages2–4 were rendered and visually inspected. Figure6 resolves the span sequence64+128+200+128+72m with26+24+24m eastern approaches. Figures7–8 show the14m box and10.5m wings; figure13 shows the three-rod hanger and anchor hardware. Source paper is CC-BY4.0; no figure or photograph is embedded in the model.
- [Da Nang city description](https://cttdt.danangportal.gov.vn/vi/w/cau-rong-bieu-tuong-kien-truc-moi-trong-thoi-ky-hoi-nhap-i): current landmark identity and steel dragon components.
- [Exact map feature](https://www.openstreetmap.org/way/694831926): Q5305270, heading and original coarse outline; attribution retained in map-frame.json.

Public Esri aerial imagery was inspected in a browser. Its coordinate extent and visually identified supports are recorded in map-frame.json. The mapped polygon includes roughly100m of additional approach road; authored geometry uses the published666m structure. Origin shift is provisional pending closer endpoint verification. The pier waterline reference and deck vertical curve need terrain review.

The head is reconstructed from layered cut steel photographs, with open mouths and curled appendages; it requires comparison against current model closeups. Bolt dimensions, streetlight profiles, underside bracing and rail pitch are photographic reconstruction. Effects such as water and fire are outside this static asset.
