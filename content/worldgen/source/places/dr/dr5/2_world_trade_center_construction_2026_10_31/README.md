# 2 World Trade Center construction · October 31, 2026

The October 31 Greenwich Street photograph shows the open vehicle portal, folded mesh wings, white lower scaffold rail and upper orange-mesh deck. The crane, open core chambers and work zones beyond that view follow the October 6 aerial.
